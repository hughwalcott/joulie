# Resources for further information — interactive tools and calculators

When a household wants to put numbers on their own situation, point them to these **official, first-party interactive tools** on the websites in this corpus. All of them are non-commercial (government-run) or first-party, and all produce **indicative estimates only** — actual results depend on each home's retailer, plan, usage and region.

## EECA / Gen Less (energy-saving tools)

- **Home energy savings calculator** — models savings across the five highest-impact areas (heating, hot water, cooktop, vehicles, and rooftop solar), personalised by household size and location. Includes a solar-savings module (assumes a 5 kW north-facing system unless you use the dedicated solar tool). Data model last updated March 2025.
  - https://www.eeca.govt.nz/for-homes/solar-for-homes/solar-power-calculator/
  - (also on Gen Less: https://www.genless.govt.nz/for-everyone/at-home/energy-saving-appliances/home-energy-savings-calculator/ )
- **Efficient appliance calculator** — compares the energy efficiency and running costs of regulated products, using efficiency ratings supplied by NZ manufacturers/importers.
  - https://www.eeca.govt.nz/for-homes/energy-saving-technology/efficient-appliance-calculator/
- **Warmer Kiwi Homes — check your eligibility** — grants of 50–90% off insulation and up to 90% off heating for eligible households; eligibility can be checked online.
  - https://www.eeca.govt.nz/co-funding-and-support/products/warmer-kiwi-homes-programme/check-eligibility/ (follow "Warmer Kiwi Homes — check your eligibility")

## Rewiring Aotearoa (household electrification tools)

- **Electric calculator** — plug in your own energy use, machines, driving and hot water to see personalised savings and emissions from electrifying, and where to focus next.
  - https://www.rewiring.nz/calculator
- **Make a Plan (electrification to-do list)** — builds a staged, downloadable plan timed to when each machine reaches end of life.
  - https://www.rewiring.nz/plan

## Electricity Authority (market and connection tools)

- **Billy** — the Authority's free, independent power-plan comparison and switching site (owned/operated by the EA, no commissions; models from real household data). Upload a bill or answer a few questions to see potential savings, with a time-of-use filter.
  - https://www.billy.govt.nz/
- **Your meter (ICP lookup)** — enter your address to find your ICP number, your power company, and your meter type (useful before switching or going solar).
  - https://www.ea.govt.nz/your-power/your-meter/
- **Solar installations map** — interactive map of solar (and battery) uptake across New Zealand.
  - https://www.ea.govt.nz/data-and-insights/charts-and-dashboards/solar-installations-map/
- **Regional power prices** — dashboard of average bills by region, to compare your charges against the regional average.
  - https://www.ea.govt.nz/data-and-insights/charts-and-dashboards/regional-power-prices/
- **Shift to 10kW export limits** — dashboard tracking each distributor's residential export-limit position (relevant before installing solar/battery).
  - https://www.ea.govt.nz/data-and-insights/charts-and-dashboards/shift-to-10kw-export-limits/

## MBIE (official data and modelling)

- **Energy in New Zealand — data tables** — the authoritative annual statistics, with full Excel data tables (generation mix, prices, renewables, gas, oil).
  - https://www.mbie.govt.nz/building-and-energy/energy-and-natural-resources/energy-statistics-and-modelling/energy-publications-and-technical-papers/energy-in-new-zealand
- **Electricity Demand and Generation Scenarios (EDGS)** — scenario modelling of future demand and generation to 2050 (with downloadable assumptions/results files).
  - https://www.mbie.govt.nz/building-and-energy/energy-and-natural-resources/energy-statistics-and-modelling/energy-modelling/electricity-demand-and-generation-scenarios
- **Interactive Levelised Cost of Electricity (LCOE) comparison tool** — ranks generation projects by long-run cost. *Note: this is a downloadable spreadsheet (XLSM) aimed at analysts, using 2021 default assumptions and marked illustrative — not a household bill calculator.*
  - https://www.mbie.govt.nz/building-and-energy/energy-and-natural-resources/energy-statistics-and-modelling/energy-modelling/interactive-levelised-cost-of-electricity-comparison-tool

## Commerce Commission

- The Commission publishes **interactive graphics and information-disclosure summaries** on lines-company performance (revenue, reliability), plus a short animated explainer of its role. These are performance-transparency tools rather than household calculators.
  - https://www.comcom.govt.nz/regulated-industries/electricity-lines/

## Referenced external tool (not part of this corpus)

- **NIWA SolarView** — estimates sunlight hours/solar potential for a specific roof; referenced by both the Rewiring and EA solar pages. Hosted by NIWA (a Crown research institute), so it sits outside the sites processed here — flagged for completeness, not ingested.
  - https://solarview.niwa.co.nz/

## How Joulie should use these

Offer the **single most relevant** tool for the question (e.g. "compare power plans" → Billy; "should I get solar?" → EECA home energy savings calculator or Rewiring's electric calculator; "check my meter/ICP" → Your meter). Always add that results are **indicative estimates**, and that for anything binding the household should confirm with their retailer, lines company or a qualified installer. Do not steer users to any particular power company or product.

# Energy in New Zealand (official energy statistics — overview)

**Energy in New Zealand** is MBIE's flagship annual publication — the comprehensive, authoritative record of New Zealand's energy **supply and demand** for a calendar year. It's the primary government source for the numbers that appear (sometimes second-hand) throughout the other corpora, so Joulie should treat it as the reference of record and cite the edition/year.

## Latest headline figures (2024 calendar year — "Energy in New Zealand 2025")

- The **renewable share of total primary energy supply reached a record 45.5%** in 2024, growing each year since 2020 on strong geothermal, solar and wind growth.
- **85.5% of electricity was generated from renewable sources** in 2024 — down from 88.1% in 2023 — because low hydro inflows and declining gas supply meant more coal was burned.
- **Total energy consumption fell 2.1% to 524.8 PJ**, mainly on lower industrial demand.
- **Natural gas consumption fell to its lowest since 2011** (ongoing field depletion and lower supply).
- **National electricity generation** rose 0.9% to **43,879 GWh**; **hydro fell to 23,490 GWh** (lowest since 2013); **coal generation rose 118% to 2,243 GWh** (5% of generation, up from 2%).
- **Renewable generation capacity grew 556 MW** in 2024 (up 17%, or 1,262 MW, since 2020).

## How electricity is used

Roughly **a third of New Zealand's electricity is used by households**, about **a third by industry** (mostly basic metals, food processing, and wood/pulp/paper), and about **a quarter by the commercial sector**, with the remainder in agriculture and transport. The **Tiwai Point aluminium smelter is the single largest user** of electricity in the country.

## What's in the publication (sub-topics)

Each edition breaks down into: **Electricity, Oil, Gas, Petroleum reserves, Renewables,** and **Prices** statistics, plus balanced energy supply-and-demand tables (which show supply, demand, and the losses/inefficiencies of converting one fuel type to another).

## Accessing the data

Full data tables are published as **Excel files** on the source page, alongside each year's PDF (editions back to 2013 are available). The older interactive **New Zealand Energy Dashboard was discontinued in January 2024**; data now lives in the Excel files and the topic pages. MBIE also publishes the **New Zealand Energy Quarterly** for more frequent updates, and runs a **data release calendar** and an energy-data mailing list (energyinfo@mbie.govt.nz).

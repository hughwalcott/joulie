# Data: Solar installations map

An interactive map showing **solar installations across New Zealand** — where systems are located, how large they are, and what's already operating in a given area. It lets a household see solar uptake in their neighbourhood.

## Context figures

At the time of the 2026 export-limit changes, about **75,000 households had rooftop solar**, and more than **14,700 of those also had batteries** — a small but fast-growing share of roughly two million connections, which is why network export rules and distributed-generation pricing are becoming more important. For a household considering solar, the map is a way to gauge local adoption; the SEANZ Solar Optimiser and EECA Solar power calculator (linked from the EA "Solar power" page) are the tools for sizing a system.

# Data: Disconnections for non-payment

This dashboard publishes the **monthly data on electricity disconnections for non-payment** — a consumer-protection indicator that the Authority tracks alongside the Consumer Care Obligations (see Part 11A and the consumer-facing hardship page).

## What it shows and why it matters

It reports how many residential connections are **disconnected for non-payment** each month, letting the Authority and the public monitor whether hardship protections are working and whether disconnections are trending up or down. It complements a related access measure: in 2025, power companies **turned down around 46,900 applications for post-pay electricity for credit-related reasons** — many of those households end up on **prepay**, where supply simply stops when credit runs out (a particular risk for medically dependent households).

Together these figures frame the equity dimension of home electrification: the Consumer Care Obligations restrict when a retailer can disconnect a post-pay customer (and prohibit disconnecting known medically dependent consumers), while the disconnection and access data show how many people are still affected. If you're struggling to pay, contact your retailer early (see "If you're finding it hard to pay your bill").

# Data: Regional power prices

This dashboard shows the **average electricity bill by region** so a household can check its fixed and variable charges against the regional average and see whether it could get a better deal (via Billy, billy.govt.nz).

## What the data is

It's built from data collected under the **clause 2.16 retail market monitoring notice** — retailers report their **actual billing and tariffs** charged to residential customers, including plans that aren't generally available (like legacy plans). Key notes:

- All prices **include GST, except the lines component**.
- The **average fixed tariff** and **average variable tariff** are each an **ICP-weighted average** across a region for a user type; they're calculated independently and may not match any actual plan being offered.
- Data is **subject to revision** and updated **monthly** after retailers submit (so the most recent calendar month lags by a month or two).

## Why prices vary

- **Generation costs** — where and how electricity is produced (locational pricing and price separation).
- **Lines charges** — set by local lines companies (and regulated by the Commerce Commission).
- **User type** — whether you're on a **low user** or **standard user** plan. Under the **Low Fixed Charge regulations**, retailers must offer low-user plans, which have **maximum fixed (daily) charges**.
- **Consumption** — household size, heating source, fuel options, local climate and season.

The Authority notes recent lines-charge increases hit regions unevenly — average households saw around **$10/month** increases while some rural regions faced up to **$25/month**. The practical prompt is the same: call your retailer to ask if you're on its cheapest plan for your use, or compare on Billy.

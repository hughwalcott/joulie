# Consumer Care Obligations (your rights with your power company)

The **Consumer Care Obligations** are mandatory rules (set out in Schedule 11A.1 of the Electricity Industry Participation Code 2010) that require your power company to look after residential customers. This page is the consumer-facing overview; two dedicated pages in this corpus cover the medically dependent and payment-difficulty situations in detail.

## What your power company must do

- **Not disconnect you** if it knows you or someone in your household is a **medically dependent consumer**.
- **Support you** if you're having trouble paying your bill.
- Keep any **fees or charges reasonable**.
- Have a **consumer care policy** explaining how it meets these obligations, published somewhere easy to find on its website.

You can also nominate a **support person** and an **alternative contact person** (confirm with your company that they have your permission to act for you). Find your company's contact details on your bill or website, or via the "Your meter" tool.

## When you sign up to a new power company

Before you sign up, you're entitled to clear information about the plans on offer, what they mean, and your rights before and after becoming a customer — so you know what protections are in place.

## If your power company doesn't meet its obligations

If you think your company hasn't met the Consumer Care Obligations (for example, around a disconnection), you can **raise a concern with the Electricity Authority**. You can also contact **Utilities Disputes**, a free, independent dispute-resolution service.

## Background (why these exist)

Following the 2020 Electricity Price Review, the Authority replaced its older medically-dependent and vulnerable-consumer guidelines with voluntary Consumer Care Guidelines. A 2023 review found these were applied inconsistently across the market, so the Authority made them **mandatory Consumer Care Obligations from 2025**. Two key protections — not disconnecting known medically dependent consumers, and keeping fees/charges reasonable — took effect **1 January 2025**; the remaining obligations took effect **1 April 2025**. The Authority publishes an annual report on retailers' compliance.

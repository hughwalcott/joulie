# If you're finding it hard to pay your bill

It can be hard to pay bills from time to time. The Consumer Care Obligations require your power company to work with you — so the most important step is to **tell your power company as soon as possible**, and they can help find a suitable payment plan and point you to further support.

## Protections before any disconnection

If you're a **post-pay** customer, before your company can disconnect you for non-payment it must:

- make sure **no one in your home needs power to prevent serious harm to their health**;
- follow the required **missed-payment process** (for example, sending reminder notices and offering support); and
- try to **contact you at least five times** (including after you miss a payment).

Your company must also make **every reasonable effort to help you stay connected**, and must reconnect you as soon as possible if it cuts you off by mistake or once you pay.

## Medically dependent households

Your power company **cannot disconnect** you if your household includes a medically dependent consumer who is registered — or is applying to be registered — even for non-payment. You are still required to pay your bills. (See the medically dependent consumers page in this corpus.)

## Other support

Talk to your retailer about payment arrangements early — whether you're in difficulty now or can see it coming. You can also check whether a cheaper plan is available using Billy (billy.govt.nz), and seek budgeting support from community services.

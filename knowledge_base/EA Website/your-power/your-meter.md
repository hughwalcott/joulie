# Your meter (ICP lookup)

"Your meter" is an Authority lookup tool: enter your address to find your **ICP number**, your power company, and what type of meter you have.

## What an ICP is

Your **Installation Control Point (ICP) number** uniquely identifies your property as an individual power connection and tells you how your property is connected to the electricity network. You can find it on your electricity bill or via the address search. It's especially useful when **switching power companies**, so your supply isn't confused with any other connection.

## Practical notes

- The ICP address is the **distributor's supply address**, which may not exactly match your postal or street address. For a unit/flat, try the format like "150-10 Road name".
- If your address details look wrong, contact your power company to request a change — the Authority doesn't maintain the underlying source data.
- For many addresses or ICPs at once, the Authority offers an ICP lookup **API** for bulk searches.

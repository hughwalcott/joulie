# How to get cheaper power bills with a time-of-use plan

On a **flat-rate** plan the price per unit doesn't change through the day. On a **time-of-use (TOU)** plan you can get cheaper — or even free — power during off-peak times, so shifting when you use electricity can lower your bill. This is directly relevant to home electrification: EVs, hot water heat pumps and other big electric loads can often be shifted to the cheapest hours.

## Will a time-of-use plan save me money?

TOU plans work best for households that **can shift their electricity use to off-peak times and stay motivated** to do so. If you use a lot of power — a spa pool, swimming pool or electric hot-water cylinder — and can run those off-peak, savings can be significant. They're **particularly suited to EV owners** who charge overnight when rates are lowest (about 70% of EV owners already do this). They may not suit households that can't change their habits much (e.g. young kids needing power at all hours, or homes that heat with gas).

## How to save the most

Savings depend on how much load you can move to cheaper times. Easy shifts:
- Charge your EV overnight or on weekends.
- Load the dishwasher, dryer and washing machine and use the delay-start, or run them just before bed.
- Put timers on hot-water cylinders, spa pools and swimming pools so they run in cheaper periods.

## Why prices vary through the day

Electricity isn't easy to store, so it's generated when needed. At **peak** times (e.g. weekday mornings) demand is high and the system relies on more expensive generation, so prices rise. **Off-peak** (overnight, weekends) it's cheaper to meet lower demand, and retailers can buy in bulk at lower rates and pass the savings on through TOU plans.

## Typical price periods

Bands vary by retailer, but a TOU plan typically has three:
- **Peak (most expensive):** ~7am–10am and ~5pm–9pm.
- **Off-peak / shoulder (mid-price):** ~10am–5pm and ~9pm–11pm.
- **Night (cheapest):** ~11pm–7am.

## Finding a time-of-use plan

Use **Billy** (billy.govt.nz), the Authority's free price-comparison and switching website; its results page has a filter to show the TOU plans available at your address. (Billy is the government's independent comparison tool — see also the "Compare and switch" page in this corpus.)

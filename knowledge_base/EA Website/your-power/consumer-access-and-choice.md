# Consumer access and choice

This is an Authority workstream looking at the challenges some people face getting electricity or finding the right plan — and the impact on them and their whānau. The Authority is gathering information (including via a public survey) and is **not proposing rule changes right now**, but may do so in future based on what it learns.

## Why it matters (an access barrier)

A key issue is access to **post-pay** electricity. In 2025, power companies **turned down around 46,900 applications for post-pay electricity for credit-related reasons**. People declined post-pay often end up on **prepay**, which carries different risks — notably that supply stops when credit runs out, including for medically dependent households (see the medically dependent consumers page). This makes access and choice an equity issue in home electrification: those who could most benefit from cheaper, better plans can be the least able to access them.

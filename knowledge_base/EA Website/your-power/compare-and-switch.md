# Compare and switch to save (Billy)

Switching power companies is quick and easy and could save money — the key is being on the right plan for your household's needs. The Authority runs **Billy** (billy.govt.nz), a free, independent price-comparison and switching website that compares power plans and companies to help you find the best deal.

## Check your meter first

You may need a **smart meter** to move to certain plans or companies. For example, time-of-use plans with a "free hour of power" or reduced night rates rely on **half-hourly data** from a smart meter. Use the Authority's "Your meter" tool to check whether you have one; if not, your power company may be able to install one (there may be a cost).

## Related billing reforms

The Authority's billing changes reinforce switching: from 30 October 2026, retailers must present bills in plain language, must **check annually that residential customers are on the best plan the retailer offers** (and help them move at no cost if not), and bills will prompt people toward the comparison/switching website. See the "Your power bill" page in this corpus.

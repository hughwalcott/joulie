# Code Part 10 — Metering (plain-language summary)

Part 10 of the Code sets the rules for **metering** at connection points. Through it, the Authority regulates the **standards, installation, testing, accuracy, reading and data-security** requirements for meters, so that meters stay accurate and the granular data from smart meters stays confidential. (The Authority does **not** mandate smart meters or specify which type is installed.)

## Key points relevant to consumers

- **Retailers must ensure an accurate, certified meter** is in place at your property, and may need to replace or recertify meters to keep them fit for purpose.
- **Metering equipment providers (MEPs)** own/operate meters and are responsible under the Code for **protecting your consumption data** — data carries an **ICP identifier**, not your name or address, and can only be disclosed to you or people you authorise.
- **Smart meters** record **half-hourly** use and transmit it to your retailer, enabling accurate (not estimated) billing and access to **time-of-use** plans; this underpins solar buy-back (import/export metering) and easier switching.

For the consumer-facing explanation, see the "Meters" and "Your meter" pages in this corpus.

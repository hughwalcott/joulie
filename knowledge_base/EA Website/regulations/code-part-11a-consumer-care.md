# Code Part 11A — Consumer Care (plain-language summary)

Part 11A (with the obligations set out in **Schedule 11A.1**) contains the mandatory **Consumer Care Obligations** that retailers owe residential customers. It's the legal backing for the consumer-facing pages in this corpus. The obligations were phased in during 2025 (two key protections from **1 January 2025**, the rest from **1 April 2025**).

## Who and what it covers

A **customer** is a residential consumer who has a contract with a retailer for electricity used wholly or partly for residential purposes. Retailers must:

- **Publish a consumer care policy** explaining how they meet the obligations.
- Provide terms and conditions in **plain English**.
- Keep any **fees and charges reasonable**.
- Proactively **collect and use customer information** to support customers, especially those who may face payment difficulty (including recording communication-preference and support needs).

## Payment difficulty and disconnection (Parts 6/10 of the Obligations)

Before disconnecting a **post-pay** customer for non-payment, a retailer must (among other things): have the contractual right to disconnect; follow the required missed-payment process (reminders, offers of support); make a set number of **contact attempts**; meet extra conditions if the invoice used an **estimated (not actual) meter reading**; and **satisfy itself that no one at the premises is a medically dependent consumer**. Retailers must remain responsive about reconnection and reconnect as soon as reasonably practicable once resolved.

## Medically dependent consumers (a key protection)

A retailer **must not** disconnect a post-pay customer's premises where it knows a **medically dependent consumer** may permanently or temporarily reside (except emergency disconnections), and must **notify the Authority** as soon as it becomes aware of such a disconnection. A person's status is confirmed via a **confirmation-of-status form** completed by a health practitioner. If a retailer isn't the trader responsible for the ICP, it must still share medically-dependent information with the responsible trader.

## Compliance

Retailers submit annual **Consumer Care Obligations compliance reports** to the Authority, which publishes findings. Consumers who believe a retailer hasn't met the obligations can raise a concern with the Authority or contact **Utilities Disputes**.

# Distribution (lines companies: role, pricing, losses, obligations)

**Distributors** (also called lines companies or network companies) own and operate the **local networks** that carry electricity from Transpower's grid substations to homes and businesses. They're regional monopolies, so several rules govern how they price and operate.

## Distribution pricing

Efficient distribution pricing supports a reliable, affordable supply. Distributors must **reference the Authority's distribution pricing principles**, **publish their pricing methodology**, and **discuss their pricing plans and progress with the Authority each year** (see Code Part 6B). They're **also** subject to information disclosure and **default or customised price-quality regulation by the Commerce Commission**, which caps how fast lines charges can rise.

## Distribution losses

As electricity travels through lines, a proportion is lost as **heat** due to resistance in the wires. Distributors must adopt a methodology to **determine and allocate the losses** arising on their networks.

## Distributed generation

Distributors handle the **connection of distributed generation** (rooftop solar, batteries, small wind, micro-hydro, solar/wind farms) to their networks under **Code Part 6**. The Authority monitors and supports compliance, can take enforcement action, and may assist dispute resolution. (See the Part 6 summary for the connection process and the 10 kW default export limit.)

## Distributors' obligations

Distributors must comply with all their Code obligations. A few consumer-relevant ones:

- Provide **clear, prominent information about Utilities Disputes** (free dispute resolution) and comparison/switching services (Billy/Powerswitch).
- Where a distributor is involved in generation or retailing, **corporate separation and arm's-length rules** and information-disclosure obligations apply (Part 3 of the Act and Part 6A of the Code).
- Certain distributors must **consult affected traders** on tariff-structure changes that could materially affect traders or consumers (Part 12A).
- Distributors with connected generators/retailers must **disclose specified information** to the Authority (e.g. distributor agreements, quantities sold by connected retailers), and must enter distributed generation information into the **registry** (Part 11).

## Why this matters for home electrification

Your **lines company sets your network connection rules** for solar/battery export, drives the distribution component of your bill, and is who your installer coordinates with to connect and commission distributed generation.

# Smart meters and home energy management

## What a smart meter is
A **smart meter** is an electronic meter that automatically records a household's electricity use in **30-minute intervals** and transmits that data electronically (typically over cellular networks) to the power company — removing the need for **manual meter readings or estimated bills**. A traditional meter only shows a cumulative running total that must be read manually or estimated. The Electricity Authority notes smart meter data is **encrypted in transit** to protect privacy.

## How it supports time-of-use pricing and electrification
Because usage is recorded in half-hour blocks rather than one total, retailers can offer **time-of-use plans** charging different rates by time of day — e.g. cheaper overnight or during high-renewable periods. This gives households near real-time visibility of usage, more accurate bills, and access to pricing that rewards **shifting flexible loads — like EV charging or hot water heating — to cheaper, often cleaner, off-peak periods**. (New rules requiring retailers to offer time-of-use plans have come into effect — see the Electrify NZ record.)

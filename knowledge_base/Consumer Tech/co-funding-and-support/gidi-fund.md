# Government Investment in Decarbonising Industry (GIDI) Fund

The **GIDI Fund**, administered by EECA, co-funds private-sector projects that switch **industrial process heat away from fossil fuels** — for example replacing coal or gas boilers with **electrode boilers, heat pumps, or biomass** systems. It has run through several standard contestable funding rounds plus larger bespoke **"GIDI: Partnerships"** projects; the Partnerships stream alone has combined about **$230 million of EECA co-investment with around $860 million of private funding**.

## Example outcomes
Two large GIDI: Partnerships projects — with **New Zealand Steel and Fonterra** — together target roughly **9.3 million tonnes of lifetime CO2-equivalent abatement**: New Zealand Steel's project alone is expected to deliver about **7.2 million tonnes of abatement by 2035**, and Fonterra's about **2.1 million tonnes by 2035**, largely by switching process heat off coal and gas.

## ⚠ Currency note
GIDI funding totals and project counts are updated (roughly quarterly) on EECA's dashboard — check for the latest figures before quoting an exact dollar amount or tonnage.

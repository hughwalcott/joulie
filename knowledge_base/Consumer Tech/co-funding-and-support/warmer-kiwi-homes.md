# Warmer Kiwi Homes

Warmer Kiwi Homes is a government programme administered by **EECA** (under the Energy Efficiency and Conservation Act 2000) that grant-funds insulation and efficient heating for eligible owner-occupied homes. Over 170,000 retrofits have been delivered since it began.

## What it pays for
- **Ceiling and underfloor insulation:** grants of **50%, 80% or 90%** of the cost (up to about **$3,450**), depending on the household's eligibility category (90% highest-need, 80% mid, 50% standard). A ground moisture barrier can be included with an insulation grant.
- **Heating (heat pump):** **90% of the cost, up to $3,450**, for the highest-need households (Community Services Card / SuperGold Combo card holders or highest-need areas). Eligible households typically pay **about $400–$700** toward the heat pump. Heating grants are only for those in most need — middle-income areas get insulation only.

## Eligibility (broadly)
- **Own and live** in the home as your main residence, **built before 2008**.
- Hold a **Community Services Card or SuperGold Combo Card**, **or** live in an area EECA identifies as low-to-middle income (check the online eligibility tool).
- For an **insulation** grant: the home doesn't already have ceiling and underfloor insulation.
- For a **heat pump** grant: the home already has insulation to EECA standard (do insulation first, often grant-funded).

## Key 2026 changes
- The **50% insulation grant was extended to more middle-income households** (from January 2026) — tens of thousands more homes became eligible, so "I didn't qualify before" is worth re-checking.
- **Wood and pellet burners are no longer funded for new applications from 9 January 2026** — heat pumps are the supported heating option.

## ⚠ Currency note
Grant percentages, caps, eligible areas and funded technologies are reviewed periodically (these changed in January 2026). Always confirm current settings via EECA's eligibility tool before treating any figure as fixed.

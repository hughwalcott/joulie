# Compare home heating options and costs

## Heat pumps are the lowest-cost, lowest-emissions option
EECA identifies **heat pumps as the lowest-cost and lowest-emissions option** for general home heating, particularly for living areas that need frequent heating. For an average New Zealand household, running a heat pump for space heating costs:
- **about $122 a year less than piped (natural) gas heating**, and
- **about $352 a year less than bottled LPG heating**.

## How other options compare
- **Wood and pellet burners** are typically cheaper to run than gas heating, but **more expensive than heat pumps**.
- **Piped natural gas and bottled LPG** are the **most expensive and highest-emissions** options in EECA's comparison.
- Electric resistance heaters suit small rooms used briefly, but cost more to run than a heat pump for regular heating.

## Funding note
Warmer Kiwi Homes funds heat pumps for eligible households, but **no longer funds wood or pellet burners for new applications from 9 January 2026** — heat pumps are the supported heating option. (See the Warmer Kiwi Homes record.)

## ⚠ Currency note
Running-cost comparisons are EECA averages under stated assumptions and depend on fuel prices; confirm current figures before quoting.

# Smart EV chargers

A **smart EV charger** connects to an app so the owner can **schedule and remotely control** when and how much the vehicle charges — typically to use cheaper **off-peak** electricity (which is also more likely to come from renewable generation).

## What a smart charger can do that a basic charger or wall socket can't
- **Scheduling / off-peak charging** via an app, for lower cost and cleaner power.
- **Load balancing:** automatically reducing its own charging rate when the rest of the household is drawing a lot of power, reducing the risk of overloading the home's electrical supply.
- **Greater electrical efficiency** than a basic 3-pin plug for the same charge delivered.

## Speed
EECA states a smart wall-mounted charger operates **at least three times faster** than charging from a standard 3-pin household socket.

# EV uptake and running costs in New Zealand

## How many EVs are on the road
EECA reported that by the **end of 2023 there were over 100,000** battery electric and plug-in hybrid vehicles registered on New Zealand roads — a **57% increase on 2022**, up from fewer than 2,000 EVs when EECA began tracking EV attitudes in 2016. Uptake has continued: the Government reported EV registrations in 2026 up about 96.4% year-on-year (see the Beehive charger record).

## Are EVs cheaper to run than petrol?
Generally yes on an energy-cost-per-kilometre basis: **electricity is typically cheaper per km than petrol**, and EVs have **fewer moving parts** (no oil changes, exhaust or engine servicing in the same way), lowering maintenance. Since **1 April 2024**, though, this gap is partly offset for EV owners by **RUC** at the same rate as diesel (see the NZTA RUC record). The actual saving depends on electricity price, charging pattern (home vs public fast charging) and the vehicles compared.

## ⚠ Currency note
EV fleet numbers grow quickly — the ">100,000 by end-2023" figure is a dated milestone (marking when the RUC exemption's ~2%-of-fleet threshold was reached). For a current count use NZTA's monthly fleet statistics.

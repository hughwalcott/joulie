# Solar energy in New Zealand

Solar has historically been a small contributor to New Zealand's electricity — **around 1% of electricity consumption in 2021** — but uptake is accelerating as rooftop and grid-scale solar costs fall and utility-scale solar farms connect to the grid. EECA/industry forecasts cited by EECA suggest solar PV could supply **as much as 6% of New Zealand's electricity by 2035**, with a wide range of uncertainty depending on technology costs.

## Cross-reference (current share)
For the most recent measured share, see the MBIE generation-mix record: solar was about **1.4% of generation in 2024** and rising fast (solar generation hit a record ~601 GWh in 2024, up ~62%). Solar is the fastest-growing part of the mix.

## ⚠ Currency note
Solar is the fastest-changing part of New Zealand's generation mix. Treat any single percentage as a dated snapshot and check EECA / the Electricity Authority / MBIE for a current share before quoting an exact figure.

# What EECA is

**EECA (the Energy Efficiency and Conservation Authority)** is the Crown entity responsible for promoting **energy efficiency, energy conservation, and renewable energy** use in New Zealand, operating under the Energy Efficiency and Conservation Act 2000.

## Its role in electrification
- Administers **household and business funding programmes** such as **Warmer Kiwi Homes** and the **GIDI Fund**.
- Publishes **consumer and technical guidance** on efficient technologies (heat pumps, hot water heat pumps, induction cooktops, insulation, solar, EVs and EV charging).
- Delivers the **Equipment Energy Efficiency (E3)** product regulation programme (MEPS and Energy Rating Labels) jointly with Australia.
- Produces **national data and analysis** on energy use and the energy transition.

# Explainer — Electrification for humans (people power)

This is Rewiring's values-and-framing piece: an argument that electrification is about **people**, not just carbon or kit.

## Beyond "carbon tunnel vision"

People sometimes talk about "carbon tunnel vision" — the single-minded pursuit of emissions reductions at the expense of everything else. Rewiring argues this is the wrong way to think about electrification. It isn't only about emissions (even though preventing dangerous overheating is quite the co-benefit), and it isn't only about the technology — the rooftop solar, batteries, electrified appliances and vehicles. Those are just the *means to an end*.

The thought experiment Rewiring uses: if someone invented a machine that removed greenhouse gases at industrial scale and "fixed" climate change, would electrification still be worth doing? Their answer is an emphatic **"hell yeah"** — because electrifying everything improves human wellbeing across many dimensions at once.

## Why electrification is "a fundamentally better way to power our lives"

Compared with fossil-fuel energy, renewable energy is **cheaper, cleaner, more efficient, more abundant and more resilient** over the long run. So the electrification of everything is set to enhance human wellbeing in various ways, often with immediate benefits — lower bills, healthier homes (no combustion toxins indoors), more energy security and resilience, and money that stays in local communities rather than flowing offshore for imported fuel.

## Panoptic, not tunnel, vision

Rewiring contrasts "carbon tunnel vision" with what it calls **"panoptic vision"** — seeing the full breadth of benefits. But it adds an important caveat: whether everyone shares in those benefits depends on *how* we roll out the transition — on designing energy markets, supply chains and policy to avoid inequity and injustice. Getting ahead of the curve is what ensures the gains of electrification reach everyone, now and into the future — the essence of "people power."

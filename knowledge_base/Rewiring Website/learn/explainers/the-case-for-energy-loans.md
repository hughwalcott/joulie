# Explainer — The case for energy loans

Rewiring's argument: the biggest barrier to unlocking the household savings (and public benefits) that electric technology offers is the **upfront cost**, and long-term, low-interest energy loans are "the ladder we need to get more people over that." In Rewiring's policy manifesto, long-term low-interest loans for electric upgrades ranked as the single most effective option out of 60 ideas.

## Why ordinary bank "green loans" fall short

The promotional low-interest terms on bank green loans are much shorter than the lifespan of the machines they fund:

- Westpac's 0% offering must be repaid in full within **five years**.
- ASB (then ANZ and BNZ) now allow longer total amortisation, but the rate typically reverts to the **standard floating mortgage rate** (around 5–6%) at the end of the discounted period.
- Even when not required to, many households feel pressured to repay as much as possible during the discount window — which pushes up their bills and cost of living in the short term.

Longer loans carry a higher risk weighting for banks (more time for something to go wrong, more capital tied up), so banks favour short-term lending — exactly the opposite of what long-lived solar, batteries and appliances need.

## What a well-designed energy loan does

Unlike short bank green loans, an affordable long-term loan could deliver household savings **immediately**, because repaying the loan and interest on solar, batteries and electric machines over the long run is less than the current electricity and fuel bills. In short: it becomes **cheaper to finance the new things than to keep paying to run the old ones**. Rewiring's illustrative modelling (9 kW solar at ~$1,700/kW plus a replacement inverter, 25-year term at 4%; 10 kWh battery at ~$900/kWh, 15-year term at 4%) shows savings that grow each year as grid prices rise. A related scenario finds an average household could save almost **$15,000 over 15 years** with a 9 kW solar + 10 kWh battery system financed at 5.5%.

## The policy vehicle — Energy IMPACT loans via the RAS

Rewiring has been working with partners on **Energy IMPACT loans** delivered through a **Ratepayer Assistance Scheme (RAS)**: loans tied to the property (repaid when it sells) with flexible terms, open to every property including rentals, and without imposing debt or risk on councils. Rewiring frames the scheme's potential as "the energy equivalent of ACC or KiwiSaver" — reducing the cost of living, cutting emissions and improving resilience at once.

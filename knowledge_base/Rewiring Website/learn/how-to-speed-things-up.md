# Electrification FAQ — How to speed things up

## How can I help?

Rewiring's goal is to electrify millions of fossil-fuel machines by 2040 — so that when a machine needs replacing, New Zealanders choose the electric option. When a community electrifies, the impact compounds: lower emissions, cleaner air, a supported local economy and more collective power. A key barrier is people not knowing where to start or finding trustworthy advice, and local community groups help drive awareness and uptake, bring councils to the table, and show central government that electrification is broadly popular.

## Community groups

You can start a new group, support an existing one, or add electrification to an existing group (find groups on Rewiring's map, or get in touch). The three main areas of focus are:

1. Support communities to electrify.
2. Create smart energy users.
3. Advocate for community assets and political action, especially access to finance.

Things groups do include: community surveys and resources; induction-cooking demonstrations; speaker events, workshops and information sessions; EV test drives and open days; in-home advisory services or installer partnerships; working with councils on solar for community buildings and EV charging; incentivising electrification in new buildings; bulk buys of solar panels and heat pumps; electrification expos; and engaging local media.

## Policy asks

Rewiring encourages people to ask their MP about the barriers it campaigns on — particularly access to **low-interest, long-term finance** for household electrification (see the Finance topic), better rights and incentives so **renters** and landlords benefit, and **cost-reflective two-way tariffs** (fairer export rates, "Symmetrical Export Tariffs") so customer generation and batteries are properly valued.

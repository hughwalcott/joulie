# Electrification FAQ — Jobs

## What will happen to jobs in the fossil-fuel industry?

There will be job losses as new technologies take over, which Rewiring argues can be managed with good planning and training schemes. It frames renewable energy as a growth opportunity for NZ — more money staying in communities, and rising demand for workers from solar and heat-pump installers to electricians, plus incentives for entrepreneurs.

Supporting points it cites:

- Clean-energy jobs grew more than twice the rate of the overall US economy in 2023 (Yale Climate Connections), and tend to pay above-average wages.
- Rewiring America's report suggested robust electrification could create up to 3 million US jobs.
- In Australia's large solar industry, an estimated 39 jobs are directly supported for every 1 MW of solar installed.
- For NZ, Rewiring estimates ~10,000 new solar setups are installed each year; increasing that tenfold to 100,000/year could add around 4,500 solar-installation jobs, plus more demand for electricians and maintenance workers.

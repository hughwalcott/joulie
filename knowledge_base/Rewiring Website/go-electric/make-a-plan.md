# Make a Plan (household electrification plan)

"Make a Plan" (Rewiring's "Electrification To Do List") helps a household build a simple, staged plan to electrify as machines reach the end of their life — the idea being that the next purchase, whenever it comes, is electric.

## How it works

You plug in your own details and timelines, and the tool assembles a personalised plan (downloadable as a PDF). It prompts you to think about each machine's natural replacement point, for example:

- **Vehicle** — when you expect to buy your next car, and therefore the year to upgrade to an EV.
- **Water heating** — the age of your current water heater, and when to replace it with a hot water heat pump (using the calculator to estimate annual savings).
- **Renovation timing** — whether to fold electrification into a planned renovation.
- **Solar and battery** — the year you'll add rooftop solar (and perhaps a battery) to start saving on bills immediately.
- **Everyday shifts** — e.g. trying an e-bike for short trips.

## The premise

The plan is built on Rewiring's headline finding: on average, NZ homes using gas appliances and petrol vehicles could save around **$1,500 per year** (at current interest rates), or around **$4,500 per year** with a low-interest loan, by switching to electric equivalents powered by a mix of rooftop solar, a home battery and the grid — with homes that use more power, hot water and travel saving the most. Planning ahead means each replacement decision is made calmly in advance rather than in a hurry when a machine fails.

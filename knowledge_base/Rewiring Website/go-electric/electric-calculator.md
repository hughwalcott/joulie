# Electric Calculator (household electrification calculator)

Rewiring Aotearoa's electrification calculator lets a household plug in its own numbers — rather than rely on the averages used in the research and the broad electrification guides — to see personalised savings and emissions impact, and get advice on where to focus next.

## What it does

Many NZ homes still rely on fossil fuels to heat water, keep warm, cook and get around. The calculator estimates how much a household could save (and how much it could cut emissions) by upgrading those fossil-fuel machines to efficient electric equivalents fuelled by rooftop solar, batteries and the grid. It also includes the additional savings on offer for homes that invest in rooftop solar and batteries, and — for households that have already electrified some machines — advises where to focus next.

## Why it exists

Rewiring's research uses averages to show overall trends, and the guides are deliberately broad, so they may not match any particular household. As CEO Mike Casey puts it, every household has different energy needs and is at a different stage of the journey — some drive less, others use more hot water — so the calculator lets people quantify what's in it specifically for them, whether the motivation is economic, environmental or health.

Rewiring frames the national prize as an **~$11 billion a year opportunity** if households electrify, which underpins its mission to electrify as many fossil-fuel machines as possible by 2030. As with all such tools, results are indicative and depend on the inputs.

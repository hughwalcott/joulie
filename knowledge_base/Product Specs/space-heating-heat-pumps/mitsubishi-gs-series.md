# Mitsubishi Electric GS Standard Series (high-wall heat pump)

The Mitsubishi Electric GS Standard Series is a range of single-split high-wall (air-to-air) heat pumps — one indoor unit paired with one outdoor unit — for heating and cooling individual rooms, and among the most common heat pumps in New Zealand homes. It uses R32 refrigerant and inverter compressors ("EcoCore" technology). Specifications here are from Mitsubishi Electric NZ's heat pump range; star ratings follow New Zealand's Seasonal Energy Rating (ZERL) scheme, and MEPS/registration data can be verified in the trans-Tasman Energy Rating database.

## Range and performance

The series spans six capacities. Rated heating/cooling capacity, coefficient of performance (COP, heating), energy efficiency ratio (EER, cooling) and NZ cold-zone heating star rating:

- **GS25** — 3.1 kW heat / 2.5 kW cool, COP 3.97 / EER 4.24, ~2.0★ (cold-zone heat), 23 dB(A) indoor.
- **GS35** — 3.7 / 3.5 kW, COP 3.94 / EER 3.68, 23 dB(A) indoor.
- **GS50** — 5.5 / 5.0 kW, COP 3.85 / EER 3.60, 33 dB(A) indoor.
- **GS60** — 6.6 / 6.0 kW, COP 3.88 / EER 3.61.
- **GS71** — 8.0 / 7.1 kW, COP 3.64 / EER 3.24.
- **GS80** — 9.0 / 7.8 kW, COP 3.53 / EER 3.25.

Each pairs a MSZ-GSxxVFD indoor unit with a MUZ-GSxxVFD outdoor unit. The GS is a "standard" (mild-climate-oriented) range; heating output falls as outdoor temperature drops, and its cold-zone star ratings are modest — for cold or frost-prone regions a hyper-heating range is generally preferred.

## Star ratings (New Zealand ZERL)

Star ratings are calculated under New Zealand's Seasonal Energy Rating standard (GEMS 2019) and differ by climate zone — the same model earns different stars in cold, average and hot zones. For example the GS25 rates about 2.5★ heating in cold and average zones and 3.0★ in hot zones. Use the cold-zone rating for southern/inland NZ.

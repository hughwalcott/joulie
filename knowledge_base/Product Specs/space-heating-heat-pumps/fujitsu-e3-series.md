# Fujitsu e3 Series (high-wall heat pump)

The Fujitsu e3 Series is a range of single-split high-wall (air-to-air) inverter heat pumps for room heating and cooling, using R32 refrigerant, and one of the most widely sold heat pump ranges in New Zealand. Specifications here are from Fujitsu's NZ e3 Series datasheet; star ratings follow the AU/NZ energy rating (GEMS) scheme and can be verified in the trans-Tasman Energy Rating database.

## Range and performance

Rated heating/cooling capacity, COP (heating), EER (cooling) and star ratings across common sizes:

- **ASTG09** (2.5 class) — 3.2 kW heat (range 0.9–5.3) / 2.5 kW cool, COP 4.92 / EER 4.90, 5.0★ / 5.0★, from 21 dB(A) indoor. The most efficient in the range.
- **ASTG12** (3.5 class) — 3.7 / 3.5 kW, COP 4.57 / EER 4.07, 4.5★ / 3.5★.
- **ASTG18** (5.0 class) — 6.0 / 5.0 kW, COP 4.23 / EER 4.10, 3.5★ / 3.5★.
- **ASTG24** (6.0 class) — 7.2 / 6.0 kW, COP 4.07 / EER 3.77, 3.5★ / 3.0★.
- **ASTG30** (7.1 class) — 8.0 / 7.1 kW, COP 3.77 / EER 3.53, 3.0★ / 2.5★.

As is typical, the smaller units are the most efficient and highest-rated; efficiency and star rating fall as capacity rises. Fujitsu offers a 6-year parts-and-labour warranty when installed by an accredited installer.

## Note for corpus maintainers

*As with all air conditioners, COP/EER are single-point rated figures; the star rating and seasonal/annual figures better reflect real performance and are the MEPS-relevant numbers. Air conditioners are MEPS-regulated trans-Tasman, so confirm the exact model (indoor ASTGxx + matched outdoor AOTGxx) and its registered star ratings in the Energy Rating database (energyrating.gov.au). Model codes and the exact capacity/COP within a "class" have changed across e3 revisions (e.g. ASTG/ASTH KMCA/KMTA/KMTD suffixes) — some figures above are from an NZ e3 datasheet and should be reconciled to the current model year. Star ratings can differ by climate zone under the NZ ZERL scheme. Datasheet undated in retrieved copy; retrieval date used.*

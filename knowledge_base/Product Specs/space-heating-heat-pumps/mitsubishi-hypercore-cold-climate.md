# Mitsubishi Electric HyperCore (cold-climate heat pump)

HyperCore is Mitsubishi Electric's cold-climate heat pump technology for New Zealand — designed so the unit delivers its **full rated heating capacity right down to −15 °C** outdoor ambient, and continues heating even while snowing. It is the counterpart to the "standard" GS-type ranges (documented separately), which are oriented to milder climates. Specifications here are from Mitsubishi Electric NZ; air conditioners are MEPS-regulated trans-Tasman, so registered figures can be verified in the Energy Rating database.

## Why cold-climate performance matters

An ordinary heat pump starts to produce less heat below about 7 °C outdoor ambient, and the drop is pronounced below 0 °C, because there is less ambient heat to recover from cold air and the unit spends more time defrosting. Mitsubishi's own sizing guidance illustrates the effect: a nominally 6.8 kW unit might deliver only around 5.78 kW at 0 °C, so units must not be undersized for cold conditions — or a HyperCore model chosen. HyperCore guarantees full rated output to −15 °C via larger heat-exchange area, a more capable compressor, and optimised defrost logic. For much of the South Island and cold inland North Island locations, this is the appropriate class rather than a standard range.

## Representative range

HyperCore is offered as an option/variant across several indoor styles (high wall, floor console). The MSZ-LN premium high-wall series is a common HyperCore-capable range:

- **LN25** — 3.2 kW heat / 2.5 kW cool.
- **LN35** — 4.0 / 3.5 kW.
- **LN50** — 6.0 / 5.0 kW.
- **LN60** — 6.8 / 6.1 kW.

Indoor sound levels start from about 19 dB(A) on the lowest fan setting, among the quietest in New Zealand.

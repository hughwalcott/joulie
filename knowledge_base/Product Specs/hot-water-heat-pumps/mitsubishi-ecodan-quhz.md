# Mitsubishi Electric Ecodan QUHZ

The Mitsubishi Electric Ecodan QUHZ is a CO2 (R744) hot water heat pump comprising a 4 kW monobloc outdoor unit (QUHZ-W40VA), a dedicated pre-plumbed 200 L thermal-store cylinder (EHPT20Q-VM2EA) and an FTC5 controller. It is sold in New Zealand by Mitsubishi Electric NZ. Specifications here are from Mitsubishi Electric NZ's product pages and brochure.

## Key specifications

The system is rated at 4 kW heat output and heats domestic hot water to up to 70 °C using CO2 refrigerant (GWP 1). Mitsubishi markets it as the quietest hot-water outdoor unit in New Zealand at about 43 dB(A), with a small, lightweight outdoor unit and built-in energy monitoring as standard. Nominal water flow is 5.7 L/min. The 200 L pre-plumbed thermal store uses control logic that diverts heating to the store when its hot-water volume drops by 50 L. Rated at 4 kW, it suits a new-build home's hot water, can be retrofitted, and can also serve hot water plus room heating for a one-bedroom apartment. Mitsubishi Electric NZ provides a 5-year parts-and-labour warranty (installer training/commissioning conditions apply).

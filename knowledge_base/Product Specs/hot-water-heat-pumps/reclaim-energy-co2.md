# Reclaim Energy CO2 Heat Pump Hot Water System

The Reclaim Energy CO2 is a split-system hot water heat pump: a compact outdoor heat pump unit paired with a separate storage tank. It uses R744 (carbon dioxide) as the refrigerant — a natural refrigerant with a Global Warming Potential of 1 that performs well in cold weather. It is sold in New Zealand via Reclaim Energy NZ. Specifications here are from Reclaim's NZ CO2 HWHP design guide, cross-checked against independent Australian reviews.

## Key specifications (EHPE-4540P heat pump unit)

The heat pump unit draws about 0.916 kW to deliver 4.7 kW of thermal output, for a coefficient of performance (COP) of 5.24 measured to AS/NZS 5125:2014 (19 °C ambient air, 15 °C cold water). It heats water to 63 °C without a backup electric booster, runs on a 240 V / 50 Hz supply (10 A max), and operates from −10 °C to 43 °C ambient. The outdoor unit is notably quiet at about 37 dB at 1 m, measures 675 × 922 × 300 mm and weighs 48 kg. It pairs with tank options of 160, 250, 315 and 400 L, and can be scheduled to reheat during the day to soak up rooftop-solar generation.

## Refrigerant note

CO2 (R744) enables high water temperatures and strong cold-weather performance with a negligible climate impact if it leaks (GWP 1), versus synthetic refrigerants with GWPs in the hundreds or thousands. Reclaim also sells a newer mid-market integrated "Eco R290" all-in-one range (200 L and 300 L, propane refrigerant, COP ~4.23/4.63) — treat that as a separate record.

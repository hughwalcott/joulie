# Rinnai HydraHeat Integrated

The Rinnai HydraHeat Integrated is an all-in-one hot water heat pump — the heat pump header sits on top of the storage cylinder in a single unit — designed and built in New Zealand for local conditions. It uses R290 (propane) refrigerant and meets New Zealand's Minimum Energy Performance Standard (MEPS). Specifications here are from Rinnai NZ and NZ distributor datasheets.

## Key specifications

The HydraHeat comes in 275 L (suited to 2–5 people) and 340 L (2–7 people) capacities. It uses about 150 g of R290 refrigerant (a natural refrigerant with very low GWP) and achieves a COP of 4.7, consistent with its rated heat-pump input/output of 802 W / 3725 W under AS/NZS 5125 conditions. Hot water recovery is about 89 L/h (Eco 55 mode, 19 °C ambient, 36 °C rise). It stores water at 60 °C in standard mode (up to 70 °C in boost/high-usage), runs at about 45 dB(A) at 1 m, and operates in full heat-pump mode down to −10 °C (to +42 °C) without needing electric boosting. Six operation modes (Standard, Boost, Eco 55, Eco 50, High Usage, Element Only) and built-in Wi-Fi allow it to be tuned, including networking with grid-tied solar PV. Warranties are 5 years on the heat pump unit and 7 years on the cylinder.

## Dimensions and installation

Assembled dimensions are 1750 mm high × 692 mm diameter (275 L) or 1995 mm × 692 mm (340 L), with a 510 mm heat-pump header. Full-system weight is about 388 kg (275 L) or 465 kg (340 L). It is primarily designed for outdoor installation, delivers mains-pressure hot water, and is not suitable for spa/pool heating or hydronic use.

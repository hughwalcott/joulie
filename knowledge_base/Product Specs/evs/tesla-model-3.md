# Tesla Model 3

The Tesla Model 3 is a midsize electric sedan and a long-running NZ top-five EV seller. The 2026 update added a new Long Range RWD variant and revised the Performance model. Figures here are from Tesla New Zealand, cross-checked against EVDB NZ, EVKX and NZ motoring media; ranges are WLTP.

## Variants (2026 NZ line-up)

- **RWD (Standard)** — LFP battery (~62.3 kWh nominal, ~60.9 kWh usable), 520 km WLTP range, 210 kW rear motor, 0–100 km/h in 6.1 s, DC fast charging up to 170 kW.
- **Long Range RWD** (new for 2026) — Tesla's largest pack, an ~82 kWh usable NMC battery, delivering up to **750 km WLTP** (18" wheels) or 691 km (19") — the longest claimed range of any EV currently on sale in New Zealand. DC up to 250 kW.
- **Performance AWD** — dual-motor, ~82 kWh NMC battery, 343 kW, 571 km WLTP range, 0–100 km/h in 3.1 s.

The previous Long Range AWD was dropped for 2026. The Model 3 has an 11 kW onboard AC charger, uses CCS2 for DC charging in NZ (including Superchargers), and does not offer Vehicle-to-Load.

## Note for corpus maintainers

*Tesla does not publish headline kWh figures on its NZ configurator; battery capacities above are usable figures from EVDB NZ/EVKX. The RWD uses LFP chemistry (well suited to daily 100% charging); the Long Range and Performance use NMC. Range varies with wheel size (750 km on 18" vs 691 km on 19" for the Long Range RWD). The basic vehicle warranty changed to 5 years / unlimited km for Model 3/Y delivered from 1 January 2026. Reconcile against RightCar's official NZ energy-economy rating for the authoritative record. Prices (RWD from ~$63,900) change frequently and are indicative only.*

# MG MG4

The MG4 is a compact battery-electric hatchback and one of New Zealand's more popular EVs (2023 NZ Car of the Year). For 2026 the range was simplified and updated; a separate "Urban" model was added on a different platform. Figures here are from MG Motor New Zealand, cross-checked against EVDB NZ and NZ motoring reviews; ranges are WLTP.

## Variants (2026 NZ line-up)

- **Essence 64 (RWD)** — 64 kWh battery, single 140 kW / 350 Nm rear motor, 452 km WLTP range, 0–100 km/h in 7.5 s, DC fast charging peaking around 150–154 kW (10–80% in roughly 25 minutes).
- **XPOWER (AWD)** — 64 kWh battery, dual-motor all-wheel drive with 320 kW / 600 Nm, 405 km WLTP range, 0–100 km/h in 3.8 s, DC up to ~140 kW.
- **MG4 Urban Essence (FWD)** — a distinct 2026 model on MG's "Next Generation" platform: taller, front-wheel drive, 54 kWh battery, 118 kW / 250 Nm, 405 km WLTP range, 0–100 km/h in 8.7 s, DC up to ~150 kW, with a heat pump and battery pre-heating standard.

The MG4 supports Vehicle-to-Load, and MG NZ offers a 10-year / 250,000 km warranty when serviced through its dealer network.

## Charging and connectors

AC charging is via the onboard charger; DC fast charging peaks near 150 kW on the Essence 64 and Urban, and ~140 kW on the XPOWER. The MG4 uses the Type 2 (AC) and CCS2 (DC) connectors standard in New Zealand.

## Note for corpus maintainers

*Battery chemistry is reported inconsistently across sources: MG New Zealand's own site describes the MG4's 64 kWh pack as "NCM", while several NZ reviews (DriveLife, Tarmac Life) describe the Essence 64 pack as "LFP" and the XPOWER pack as "NCM". Record MG NZ's figure as primary but flag the conflict; confirm against the MG4's compliance/registration data for the authoritative chemistry. Pre-2026 the MG4 was sold in additional Excite/Essence 51 kWh and 64 kWh trims (now discontinued); older used examples in NZ may differ. Reconcile range against RightCar's official NZ energy-economy rating for the authoritative record.*

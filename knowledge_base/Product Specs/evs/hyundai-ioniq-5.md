# Hyundai Ioniq 5 (Series II)

The Hyundai Ioniq 5 is a midsize electric SUV on Hyundai's dedicated E-GMP platform, notable for its 800-volt architecture that enables very fast DC charging. The current NZ line-up is the Series II. Figures here are from Hyundai New Zealand, cross-checked against EVKX and Hyundai's global material; ranges are WLTP.

## Variants (NZ Series II line-up)

- **Standard Range 2WD** — 63 kWh battery, up to 440 km WLTP range, 125 kW / 350 Nm rear motor, 0–100 km/h in about 8.5 s.
- **Extended Range 2WD** — 84 kWh battery, up to 570 km WLTP range (19" wheels), the longest-range variant.
- **Extended Range AWD** — 84 kWh battery (~80 kWh usable), dual motors with 239 kW / 605 Nm, up to 546 km WLTP range, 0–100 km/h in 5.3 s.
- **Ioniq 5 N** — the performance flagship: dual-motor AWD up to 478 kW / 770 Nm, 84 kWh battery, ~448 km WLTP range.

All use NMC battery chemistry, Type 2 (AC) and CCS2 (DC) connectors, and offer Vehicle-to-Load (around 3.6 kW). Select variants offer a solar roof that trickle-charges the battery.

## Charging (800-volt advantage)

The 800-volt system supports DC "hyper-charging" up to 350 kW, taking the battery from 10% to 80% in as little as 18 minutes on a suitable charger. AC home charging covers 10–100% in roughly 8 hours. The AWD variant's peak DC rate is around 260 kW (per EVKX); the headline 350 kW / 18-minute figure applies to the Extended Range on a 350 kW charger.

# Kia EV6

The Kia EV6 is a midsize electric SUV sharing Hyundai/Kia's 800-volt E-GMP platform with the Hyundai Ioniq 5, but tuned and styled distinctly. It has been a consistent NZ top-five EV seller. For the current model year the battery was upgraded to a fourth-generation 84 kWh pack. Figures here are aggregated from Kia NZ dealer/CarExpert NZ data and cross-checked against EVKX; ranges are WLTP.

## Variants (current NZ line-up)

- **EV6 RWD** — upgraded 84 kWh battery, single 168 kW rear motor, up to 528 km WLTP range, 800-volt architecture enabling 10–80% DC charging in about 18 minutes on a 350 kW charger.
- **EV6 AWD** — dual-motor all-wheel drive, up to about 506 km WLTP range.
- **EV6 GT** — the performance flagship: dual-motor AWD with roughly 447 kW / 740 Nm, ~450 km WLTP range, 0–100 km/h in 3.5 s, DC peak ~260 kW.

All use NMC chemistry, an 11 kW onboard AC charger, Type 2 (AC) and CCS2 (DC) connectors, Vehicle-to-Load (~3.6 kW), and up to 1,600 kg braked towing. Kia NZ's warranty is 7 years / 150,000 km.

## Note for corpus maintainers

*The EV6 was updated to the 84 kWh fourth-generation battery for the current model year; the earlier NZ generation (2022–2024) used 58 kWh (Air standard-range) and 77.4 kWh (Air long-range, Earth AWD, GT-Line AWD) packs — keep used examples distinct. GT power is quoted as 447 kW (EVKX) and sometimes ~478 kW in other markets; range and power vary by wheel size and market homologation.

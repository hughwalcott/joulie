# Fisher & Paykel 60cm 4-Zone Induction Cooktop (CI604 series)

Fisher & Paykel is a New Zealand appliance brand, and its 60 cm four-zone induction cooktops (CI604 series) are widely sold here. Induction cooktops are **not** MEPS-regulated in New Zealand/Australia, so there is no government registry for them — specifications come from the manufacturer's spec sheet. Figures here are for a representative CI604 model (e.g. CI604CTPB1).

## Key specifications

The cooktop has four cooking zones on a 600 mm-wide black ceramic-glass surface: two 3000 W zones (front-left and rear-right) and two 1400 W zones (front-right and rear-left), with a SmartZone that pairs two zones into one larger cooking area for oversized cookware. It runs on 230 V with a total connected load of about 4.4 kW at 19.1 A. Because the summed zone maxima exceed the connected load, the cooktop manages/shares power across zones rather than running every zone at full power simultaneously. Touch&Slide electronic controls give immediate response, PowerBoost delivers very high heat (boil in under a minute on some zones) and GentleHeat very low heat for tasks like melting chocolate. Safety features include a keylock, safety time-out, hot-surface indicators, spillage auto-off and automatic pan detection. External dimensions are 600 × 61 × 530 mm (W × H × D).

# Haier 60cm 4-Zone Induction Cooktop — Low Current (HCI604 series)

Haier (part of the Fisher & Paykel Appliances group) sells budget-to-mid induction cooktops widely in New Zealand. Its "Low Current" 60 cm four-zone models are notable for a specific NZ-relevant reason: they are engineered to a lower rated current so they can often reuse a home's existing cooktop wiring, avoiding a costly circuit upgrade when switching from gas or ceramic. As with all cooktops, there is no MEPS registry — specifications come from Haier NZ's product pages.

## Key specifications

The Low Current models (e.g. HCI604FTPB4, HCI604TPB3) are rated at 4.5 kW total power and 19.7 A rated current at 230 V — low enough to potentially use existing kitchen wiring. They have four zones with a Flexi Zone that pairs two zones for larger cookware, touch-and-slide controls with nine heat settings plus a Boost function, a child lock, and a cool-to-touch surface with pan detection. Some models add "Hood-to-Hob", pairing over Bluetooth with a compatible Haier rangehood so extraction matches the cook-zone power level. Haier's 60 cm induction range spans 300/500/600 Series with black or mid-grey glass finishes.

# Bosch 60cm Induction Cooktop (Series 4 / Series 6)

Bosch induction cooktops are widely available in New Zealand across Series 4 (entry) and Series 6 (mid/upper) ranges. As with all cooktops, they are not MEPS-regulated, so specifications come from Bosch's spec sheets. Figures here are for a representative 60 cm 4-zone model, anchored on the Bosch NZ spec sheet for the Series 4 PUE611BB5E and cross-checked against Series 6 NZ listings.

## Key specifications

A typical 60 cm 4-zone layout (Series 4 PUE611BB5E) provides: front-left 180 mm at 1.8 kW (max 3.1 kW), rear-left 180 mm at 1.8 kW (max 3.1 kW), rear-right 145 mm at 1.4 kW (max 2.2 kW), and front-right 210 mm at 2.2 kW (max 3.7 kW). Control is via TouchSelect (Series 4) or DirectSelect touch bar (Series 6), with 17 power levels (9 main + 8 intermediate) and a PowerBoost function adding up to about 50% more power for faster heating. Series 6 adds CombiZone (combine two zones), MoveMode and QuickStart. A Series 6 60 cm NZ listing gives a connected load of about 6.9 kW; as with any induction cooktop the summed zone maxima exceed the connected load, so power is managed across zones. Surfaces are black ceramic glass; safety features include a residual-heat indicator, childproof lock, WipeProtection and automatic safety switch-off.

## Note for corpus maintainers

*No government energy registry exists for induction cooktops (not MEPS-regulated) — the Bosch spec sheet is authoritative. Bosch's model range is large (PUE/PVS/PVQ/PIE/PXX prefixes across Series 2/4/6/8, 60/80/90 cm, standard vs FlexInduction); the file uses the Series 4 PUE611BB5E NZ spec sheet for zone detail and a Series 6 NZ listing for connected load — confirm the exact model, as connected load and zone powers vary by model and some 80/90 cm models need a higher-rated or three-phase supply. Manufacturer spec sheet is regional (en-NZ) which is the correct one to cite for New Zealand. Retrieval date used pending a dated spec-sheet revision.*

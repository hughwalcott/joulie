# LONGi Hi-MO 6 HPBC (LR5-54HT residential series)

The LONGi Hi-MO 6 is a residential PV module line using LONGi's HPBC (Hybrid Passivated Back Contact) monocrystalline cell technology, which places the electrical contacts on the rear of the cell for a clean all-black front appearance and high efficiency. LONGi is the world's largest monocrystalline wafer and module producer and is well represented in the New Zealand market. Specifications here are from LONGi's Hi-MO 6 datasheet/product pages for the 54-cell residential series, cross-checked against the ENF module directory and the Clean Energy Council approved-modules list.

## Key specifications (430 W representative model)

The 54-cell residential Hi-MO 6 series runs roughly 415–430 W; the 430 W model (e.g. LR5-54HTB-430M all-black) delivers about 21.8–22.0% module efficiency. HPBC back-contact cells give a temperature coefficient of −0.29%/°C and a positive power tolerance of 0/+3%. The module measures 1722 × 1134 × 30 mm and weighs about 20.8 kg, with an operating temperature range of −40 °C to +85 °C, and is offered in single-glass and dual-glass (X6) constructions.

## Durability and warranty

The Hi-MO 6 is certified to IEC 61215 and IEC 61730, with PID resistance and strong low-light performance. Warranty terms depend on variant: the single-glass residential module typically carries a 25-year product and 25-year power warranty, while the dual-glass X6 variant extends the power warranty to 30 years. Performance is stated at Standard Test Conditions (1000 W/m², 25 °C, AM1.5).

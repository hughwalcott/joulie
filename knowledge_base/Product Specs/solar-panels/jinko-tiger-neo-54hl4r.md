# JinkoSolar Tiger Neo N-type (54HL4R residential series)

The JinkoSolar Tiger Neo is a widely installed residential PV module line using N-type TOPCon monocrystalline cells. JinkoSolar is consistently among the largest global module shippers and is well represented in the New Zealand market. Specifications here are drawn from JinkoSolar's Tiger Neo datasheet for the 54-cell residential series (representative model JKM440N-54HL4R), cross-checked against distributor datasheets and the Clean Energy Council approved-modules list.

## Key specifications (440 W representative model)

The 54HL4R residential series spans roughly 430–470 W; the common 440 W model (JKM440N-54HL4R) delivers about 22.0% module efficiency. N-type TOPCon cells give a favourable temperature coefficient of −0.29%/°C (i.e. output falls only 0.29% per °C above the 25 °C rating point), a positive power tolerance of 0/+3%, and low light-induced degradation. The module measures 1722 × 1134 × 30 mm and weighs about 21.5 kg, and is offered in monofacial and bifacial variants.

## Durability and warranty

The Tiger Neo is certified to withstand wind loads up to 2400 Pa and snow loads up to 5400 Pa, and carries a 25-year product (materials/workmanship) warranty plus a 30-year linear power warranty, with about 1% first-year power degradation and 0.4% per year thereafter. Performance figures are stated at Standard Test Conditions (1000 W/m², 25 °C cell temperature, AM1.5) and the module is certified to IEC 61215 and IEC 61730.

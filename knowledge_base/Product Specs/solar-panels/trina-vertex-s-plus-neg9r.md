# Trina Solar Vertex S+ N-type i-TOPCon (NEG9R residential series)

The Trina Solar Vertex S+ is a residential dual-glass PV module line using N-type i-TOPCon monocrystalline cells. Trina is a major global module manufacturer well represented in the New Zealand market. Specifications here are drawn from Trina's Vertex S+ APAC datasheet (NEG9R series), cross-checked against Trina's product pages and the Clean Energy Council approved-modules list.

## Key specifications (440 W representative model)

The residential Vertex S+ (NEG9R) series spans roughly 430–460 W; the 440 W model delivers about 22.0% module efficiency (up to ~22.5% at higher wattages). N-type i-TOPCon cells give a temperature coefficient of −0.29%/°C and a positive power tolerance of 0/+5 W. The module uses a dual-glass construction (more durable and fire-resistant than a polymer backsheet), measures about 1762 × 1134 × 30 mm, and is available in monofacial and bifacial variants.

## Durability and warranty

Mechanical load ratings are up to 5400 Pa front and 4000 Pa rear. The Vertex S+ carries a 25-year product warranty and a 30-year linear power warranty, guaranteeing about 87.4% of rated output at year 30 (≈1% first-year and 0.4% annual degradation). It is certified to IEC 61215, IEC 61730, IEC 61701 (salt mist), IEC 62716 (ammonia) and UL 61730, with performance stated at Standard Test Conditions (1000 W/m², 25 °C, AM1.5).

## Note for corpus maintainers

*The Vertex S+ family spans several sub-series (e.g. NEG9R.25, NEG9R.28, NEG9RC.27) and wattages ~430–460 W; the 440 W values are representative and shift by variant (monofacial/bifacial, all-black) and datasheet revision. Some sources list a 15-year product warranty for older/base variants versus 25 years for current ones — confirm the exact variant.

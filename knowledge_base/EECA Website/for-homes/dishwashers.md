# Choose an efficient dishwasher

A more efficient dishwasher uses less electricity for the same job, lowering power bills and helping smooth national electricity peaks (which reduces reliance on fossil fuels). Dishwashers are regulated through Mandatory Energy Performance Labelling; in the year ended March 2025, appliance regulation saved 1.48 PJ of energy — about 35,000 homes' annual use and around $36 million in national benefit.

## Reading the label

The stars show efficiency at a glance, but only indicate a model is cheaper to run than similar models with fewer stars — read them alongside the **annual energy consumption (kWh)**, where lower is cheaper regardless of size. The consumption figure assumes the dishwasher is used 365 times a year on the recommended cycle. Dishwashers also carry a **Water Efficiency Label**. As an emissions comparison, a **4.5-star** dishwasher generates about **43% less** emissions than a similar **3-star** model.

## Choosing and comparing

Look for the most efficient model in your budget (more stars for a similar size and a lower annual kWh figure). Compare models sold in New Zealand with the Efficient Appliance Calculator: https://www.eeca.govt.nz/for-homes/energy-saving-technology/efficient-appliance-calculator/

# Funding

This section points households to the funding and support available to help New Zealanders stay warm, dry and save energy at home.

## Warmer Kiwi Homes

The main home-focused programme, Warmer Kiwi Homes, offers grants of **50–90% off insulation** and **up to 90% off heating** for eligible households. Eligibility can be checked online, and the programme's information page explains how it works in detail.

- Check eligibility: https://www.eeca.govt.nz/co-funding-and-support/products/warmer-kiwi-homes-programme/check-eligibility/
- Programme information: https://www.eeca.govt.nz/co-funding-and-support/products/warmer-kiwi-homes-programme/information/

## Related finance

Beyond grants, most major NZ banks offer 0–1% home energy loans for energy-performance upgrades — see "Understand home energy loans": https://www.eeca.govt.nz/for-homes/improve-your-home/understand-home-energy-loans/

*Corpus note: the Funding landing page states "up to 80% off heating," which conflicts with the Warmer Kiwi Homes information page (90% heating grant, up to $3,450, for those in most need). Per project convention the programme's own page is treated as authoritative (up to 90%).*

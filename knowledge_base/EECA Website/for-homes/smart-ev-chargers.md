# Smart EV chargers

About two-thirds of NZ EV owners charge with a 3-pin plug, but many are now installing a wall-mounted charger. A smart wall-mounted charger saves time and money compared with other options.

## Why smart chargers

- **Faster charging** — at least **3× faster** than a 3-pin plug. Smart chargers typically deliver 3.6–7 kW, versus up to 1.8 kW for the standard 3-pin charger supplied with an EV.
- **Save money** — some retailers offer EV or time-of-use plans with cheaper off-peak rates; you can programme charging start times to use them, and over time power companies will offer plans that automatically optimise charging to off-peak periods (which can vary day to day).
- **Cleaner** — off-peak charging is more likely to draw on renewable generation, and smart charging is more efficient than a 3-pin plug (less electricity for the same charge).
- **Safer** — smart chargers can "load balance" the home supply, powering down automatically when the household is using a lot of power at once, reducing overload risk — especially important with older wiring. Long cables to the vehicle should be avoided.

## Smart features

Smart chargers connect via wifi and mobile, letting you control and monitor charging from a phone — setting the target charge level, the time you want charging completed, and the conditions under which charging should slow or pause.

## Choosing one — EECA's approved list

Not all wall chargers are smart or equally efficient. EECA publishes an approved list of residential and commercial EV chargers that meet its efficiency and smart-charging requirements: https://www.eeca.govt.nz/regulations/voluntary-guidance/ev-smart-charger-approved-list/

## Installation tips

- **Check the wiring** — engage an electrician or charger specialist to assess your home's wiring.
- **Give it space** — leave room on either side so the charger doesn't overheat.
- **Height** — install 1.2–1.4 m above ground so displays are readable and reachable; check the cable can reach both sides of the car (a future car's port may be on the opposite side).
- **Placement** — keep it out of the car's path and away from doorways or trip hazards.
- **Certification** — ensure you receive a certificate of compliance, including an electrical safety certificate, from the installer.

A 3-pin plug can be used when other options aren't available, but isn't recommended long-term. If you already have a non-smart wall charger, you can still get off-peak pricing benefits by manually charging at the off-peak times set by your electricity retailer (noting these can vary day to day).

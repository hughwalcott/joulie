# Understand your energy bills

A power bill is more than numbers and charges — it's a tool for making informed decisions about energy use at home: what you're paying for, how energy use adds up, and where changes could make a difference.

## Costs that make up an energy bill

- **Daily fixed charge** — a set daily fee ($/day) to get electricity from power stations to the home through national and local power lines. It stays the same regardless of how much power is used.
- **Variable charge** — the cost, in cents per kilowatt-hour (kWh), of the electricity used during the billing period.
- **Time-of-use pricing** — some power companies charge more during peak hours (usually 7–9am and 5–9pm) and less during off-peak times.
- **Discounts and penalties** — check for prompt-payment discounts being missed, or late fees being incurred.
- **Bundled charges** — if the plan includes other utilities (gas, internet), those charges appear on the bill too.
- **Usage trends** — most power bills include a usage graph showing consumption over time; this data can reveal hidden ways to save.

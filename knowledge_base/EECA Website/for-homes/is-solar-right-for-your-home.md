# Is solar right for your home?

Solar now offers real cost savings for most NZ homes. The sun in your suburb could save more than **$1,000 a year** off power bills, wherever you live, and a new system could pay itself off in as little as **7 years** — then deliver essentially free electricity for the rest of its ~25-year lifespan, adding resilience against rising power prices. Install prices start as low as **$8,500** for a small system.

## Key factors to consider

- **When you use power** — solar only generates in daylight, so the more electricity you use during the day, the more you save. A battery can help if you use a lot of power outside daylight hours.
- **Roof or ground space** — a larger system needs more space; space limits what can be installed.
- **Roof condition** — if the roof needs replacing or major maintenance within about 10 years, do that first to avoid the cost of removing and reinstalling panels. Any roof or wiring changes should happen before going solar, done with solar in mind.

## Payback and upfront cost

The shortest payback periods are generally around **7–8 years**, but can be **10–12 years** in other situations. You need to be comfortable with an upfront cost and a wait of a few years for payback before the long-term, steady savings begin.

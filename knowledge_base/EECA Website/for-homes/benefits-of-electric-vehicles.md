# Benefits of electric vehicles

## Lower emissions

EVs emit around **90% less CO₂** than an equivalent petrol vehicle when driven in New Zealand, thanks to the highly renewable electricity grid. An EECA-commissioned lifecycle assessment (2015) confirmed EVs are better for the NZ environment than petrol or diesel vehicles — both in use and across the full lifecycle from manufacture to disposal.

## Cheaper to run

Running an electric car is significantly cheaper than an equivalent petrol vehicle, even with road user charges (RUC) factored in. Charging at home is the cheapest and most convenient option, and being electric gives more control over running costs.

## Nice to drive, low maintenance

- No gears, so full power applies the moment you touch the accelerator, with no engine noise.
- Strong hill climbers, and regenerative braking recharges the battery going downhill.
- Far fewer moving parts than a combustion engine, so less to maintain or go wrong.
- EV batteries are built to last and can be refurbished or replaced.

## Range and longer trips

Most everyday driving is easily covered by home charging. For context, Auckland to Taupō is 275 km and Auckland to Wellington is 650 km, so longer trips need a top-up along the way. EV drivers tend to plan stops around lunch, coffee or toilet breaks where public chargers are — often conveniently co-located with other facilities. There are public chargers at least every 75 km on most state highways, and the network is growing fast. If you only need long range for an occasional family holiday, renting a suitable vehicle for those trips can be economical given the running-cost savings the rest of the year.

## Impact on the grid

EECA estimates that if all NZ light vehicles were electric (a long way off), total electricity demand would rise by around **20%**. Enough renewable generation is being built to accommodate a much larger EV fleet, especially with off-peak charging, and smart charging can smooth peak demand and reduce the risk of EVs overloading the system.

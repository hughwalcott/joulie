# Choose good appliances

More efficient home appliances use less energy to do the same job, cutting power bills without changing daily routines. Heating and hot water make up around two-thirds of household energy use; the rest powers other appliances, so upgrading to efficient models when technology needs replacing makes a real impact — with immediate monthly savings and a long-term payoff over the appliance's life.

## Reading the Energy Rating Label

The label makes it easy to compare similar appliances (same size and type — e.g. two 8kg clothes dryers):

- **Stars** show efficiency at a glance: more stars means more efficient than a similar-sized model. But the star rating alone only tells you a model is cheaper to run than similar models with fewer stars — not the actual cost.
- **Annual energy consumption (kWh/year)** is the key running-cost figure. Multiply it by the price you pay per kWh (check your power bill; EECA suggests the national average of about 30c/kWh) to estimate yearly running cost. Lower kWh is better.

On a retailer's website the label image may not appear, but the star rating and energy consumption should be in the product description or specifications, and in EECA's Efficient Appliance Calculator.

## Water Efficiency Labels

Clothes washers and dishwashers also display a Water Efficiency Label alongside the Energy Rating Label.

## Secondhand appliances

Secondhand appliances aren't required to display a label. You can check the energy rating of many products up to 5 years old on the Australian Government Energy Rating website — bearing in mind an older product's real performance may fall short of the label due to wear.

## When to replace

Most major appliances last about 10 years (around 8 years for televisions).

## National benefit

In the year ended March 2025, appliance regulation in New Zealand saved businesses and consumers **1.48 PJ** of energy — equivalent to the yearly energy use of about **35,000 homes**, and around **$36 million** in national benefit.

## Compare specific models

The Efficient Appliance Calculator lets you filter and compare regulated products sold in New Zealand (details and ratings supplied by NZ manufacturers and importers): https://www.eeca.govt.nz/for-homes/energy-saving-technology/efficient-appliance-calculator/

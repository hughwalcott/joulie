# Heat water efficiently

Water heating makes up around a third of a household's energy bills, so being smart about how hot water is heated and used delivers meaningful savings over time.

## Everyday hot water tips

- **Use a cold wash more often** — a hot clothes wash can use almost 10 times more electricity than a cold wash; switching saves around 25c per wash.
- **Turn off the tap** — fill the sink with hot water rather than leaving the tap running (e.g. when shaving).
- **Shower rather than bath** — showers typically use about half the water and energy, and cost about half as much.
- **Time your showers** — cutting a shower from 15 minutes to 5 can save around 66c per shower, roughly $5 per person per week.

## Set the right temperature

Hot water should be a **minimum of 60°C at the cylinder** to prevent Legionella bacteria growth, and **no more than 55°C at the tap** to avoid scalding. Heating water hotter than needed simply adds avoidable cost.

## Leaving home for a while

If the house will be empty for an extended period, check the hot water system's manual and consider turning the cylinder off completely, if it's safe to do so.

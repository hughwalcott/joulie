# Solar Power Calculator

The Solar Power Calculator estimates the payback from installing solar at a New Zealand home. Based on the details you enter, it works out an estimated **year-one saving**, the **payback period** (how long the power-bill savings take to repay the upfront cost), and total **earnings over the system's 25-year lifespan**.

It's designed to be used alongside the home solar guides — "Is solar right for your home?", "Solar costs and savings", and "Plan your solar system" — to help you judge whether solar makes financial sense for your household before getting quotes. As with all EECA estimates, results are indicative and vary with location (sunshine hours), electricity prices, system size, and how much of the generated power you use during daylight hours.

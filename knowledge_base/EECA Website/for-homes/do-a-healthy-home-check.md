# Do a healthy home check

Warm, dry homes are healthier to live in and more energy-efficient. Tackling dampness and improving heat retention saves power and money. The starting point is an assessment of the home to find where improvements can be made — either a self-service online check, or a visit from a certified advisor who can tailor an improvement plan (many advisors offer their services free of charge).

A healthy home check covers:

- whether the home has adequate ventilation and insulation/heat retention;
- how efficient the heating, hot water and lighting systems are;
- whether a rental property meets New Zealand's healthy homes standards (https://www.hud.govt.nz/our-work/healthy-homes-standards).

## Online check: HomeFit

The HomeFit tool (https://www.homefit.org.nz/) takes you through a simple online check of your home, identifying what's good about it and what could be improved.

## In-home assessment providers

- **Community Energy Action Trust** — Home Energy Checks in and around Christchurch and neighbouring districts; some households are eligible for free checks. https://www.cea.co.nz/
- **Eco Design Advisor** — a council-funded service offering free home visits or virtual consultations for residents of Dunedin, Christchurch, Nelson, Lower Hutt, Palmerston North, New Plymouth, Tauranga and Auckland. https://www.ecodesignadvisor.org.nz/
- **Sustainability Trust** — free home energy assessments and "Warm Dry Home Assessments" within the Wellington region. https://sustaintrust.org.nz/home-energy-assessments
- **Home Performance Advisor (HPA)** — maintains a public register of certified advisors across the country. https://homeperformanceadvisor.org.nz

## Funding

Warmer Kiwi Homes offers eligible homeowners grants of 50–90% of the cost of ceiling and underfloor insulation, and up to 90% of the cost of an energy-efficient heater. Eligibility check: https://www.eeca.govt.nz/co-funding-and-support/products/warmer-kiwi-homes-programme/check-eligibility/

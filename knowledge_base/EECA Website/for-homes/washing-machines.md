# Choose an efficient washing machine

A more efficient washing machine uses less electricity to do the same job — saving money on power bills and, at a national scale, helping smooth the electricity peaks that make New Zealand more likely to draw on fossil fuels. Washing machines are one of many products regulated through labelling and energy performance requirements; in the year ended March 2025, appliance regulation saved 1.48 PJ of energy (about 35,000 homes' annual use, ~$36 million in national benefit).

## Reading the label

The stars show efficiency at a glance, but the star rating alone only tells you a machine is cheaper to run than similar models with fewer stars — pair it with the **annual energy consumption (kWh)**, where lower is cheaper. The consumption figure assumes the machine is used 365 times a year on a warm wash and the recommended cycle. Washing machines also display a **Water Efficiency Label**. As an emissions comparison, a hot wash in a 5-star machine generates about **61% less** emissions than a similar 2-star machine.

## What to look for

- **Buy the most efficient model in your budget** — more stars for a similar size, and a lower annual kWh figure.
- **Right size for your household** — as a guide, up to 6 kg suits 1–2 people, 6–8 kg for 3–4 people, and at least 8 kg for 4 or more.
- **Spin speed** — 1,000 rpm or higher removes more water and cuts drying time.
- **Useful settings** — cold-wash cycles, auto-sensing, and load-size selection help you use only the water and energy you need.
- **Hot water connection** — if you have a wetback, solar water heater or hot water heat pump, a model with both hot and cold connections is cheaper to run for warm washes.

**Front vs top loader:** top loaders are usually cheaper and wash faster, but tend to use more water and energy, making them dearer to run.

## Save while using it

Use a cold wash where you can (a warm wash every 5 loads, or hot every 10, clears detergent build-up); wait for full loads; and match the water level to the load or use auto-sensing.

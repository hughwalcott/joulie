# Use your heat pump efficiently

Heat pumps are a low-cost, efficient way to heat a home — and running one smartly, not harder, saves on power bills.

- **Keep the filter clean** — a dust-clogged filter makes the unit work harder. Vacuum or wash the removable filter about once a month over winter (more often if it's dirty).
- **Know your temperatures** — set the heat pump to **18°C or above** to fight damp and mould, but **below 21°C** to save power. Between 18 and 21 is the sweet spot; higher temperatures use more energy.
- **Spend time in the heated room** — heat pumps use the most energy getting a room to temperature, so making the most of the space you heat (often the living room) is more efficient. Close doors to rooms you don't use.
- **Don't run it 24/7** — the idea that constant running is cheaper is a myth; it uses and loses more energy overall. Run the heat pump when you're home.
- **Use the timer** — set it to warm the room just before you get home or wake up, which also avoids the temptation to crank the temperature on arriving to a cold house.
- **Insulate and block draughts** — good insulation means the heat pump works less hard; thermal curtains and draught stoppers help retain heat.
- **Get to know the settings** — fine-tune how the heat pump runs to match your routine.

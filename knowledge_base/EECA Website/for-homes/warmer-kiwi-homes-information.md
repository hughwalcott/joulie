# Warmer Kiwi Homes — insulation and heater grants

Warmer Kiwi Homes provides grants toward insulation and efficient heating for eligible homeowners, to make homes warmer, drier and cheaper to run. Eligibility can be checked online: https://www.eeca.govt.nz/co-funding-and-support/products/warmer-kiwi-homes-programme/check-eligibility/

## Insulation grant

Insulation keeps heat in during winter and out during summer.

- **50%** of the cost for eligible homeowners (standard grant).
- **90%** of the cost (up to $3,450) if you hold a Community Services Card or SuperGold Combo card, or live in a highest-need area.

On average, insulation costs about **$4,300**; with the 50% grant a household would pay about **$2,150**. A well-insulated home can save up to **$340/year** off the power bill.

## Heating grant

Heat pumps are a clean, efficient way to heat a home.

- **90%** grant (up to $3,450) if you hold a Community Services Card or SuperGold Combo card, or live in a highest-need area.
- The heating grant is only for those **most in need** — if a home is in a middle-income area, heating is **not** included.

## Eligibility

To qualify you generally need to:

- Own and live in a home **built before 2008**.
- Hold a Community Services Card or SuperGold Combo card, **or** live in an area identified as low-to-middle income (use the eligibility checker to confirm your address).
- Have a home that isn't already adequately insulated (ceiling/underfloor).

Use the checker for the full, address-specific criteria: https://www.eeca.govt.nz/co-funding-and-support/products/warmer-kiwi-homes-programme/check-eligibility/

## Savings methodology (as stated by EECA)

The up-to-$340 insulation saving uses average household energy demand from EECA's Energy End Use Database (EEUD) with 2023 MBIE fuel prices. Assumptions include: heating uses either electricity or gas, not both (wood burners excluded); average setpoints of 23°C (heating) and 65°C (hot water); and home insulation increased from the current average level to the standard for newly built NZ homes. Figures are indicative and vary by home.

# Efficient Appliance Calculator

The Efficient Appliance Calculator helps you find the most energy-efficient and cost-effective appliances for your needs. You can browse and filter products to build a shortlist and compare your options side by side, including their energy efficiency ratings and likely running costs.

The product details and efficiency ratings in the tool are supplied by New Zealand manufacturers and importers, and cover regulated product categories — including heat pumps and air conditioners, fridges and freezers, washing machines, dishwashers, dryers, and televisions and monitors. For heat pumps, the tool lets you compare models by energy efficiency rating across different climate zones.

Use it alongside the guidance in "Choose good appliances" to interpret the Energy Rating Label's star rating and annual energy consumption (kWh) figure when deciding which model offers the lowest running cost for its size.

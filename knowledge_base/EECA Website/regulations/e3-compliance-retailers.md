# E3 compliance — information for retailers

If you sell, lease, hire, or hire-purchase products covered by the Energy Efficiency (Energy Using Products) Regulations 2002, you have compliance obligations as a retailer.

## Minimum Energy Performance Standards (MEPS)

Regulated products must meet the New Zealand MEPS. Ask your supplier (the importer or manufacturer) for the information showing that the products they supply meet NZ MEPS. You can check your obligations in the legislation.

## Mandatory Energy Performance Labelling (MEPL)

Seven products have MEPL requirements. If you sell, lease, hire or hire-purchase these products, it's your responsibility to ensure the label is correctly displayed and supplied with each one. There are exemptions for minimal quantities.

## Energy Rating Label information

There's no legal requirement to include Energy Rating Label information in your own materials, but EECA recommends displaying the product's star rating and annual energy consumption to help customers choose lower-running-cost products.

# Products regulated under the E3 Programme

Under the trans-Tasman E3 Programme, a range of residential, commercial and industrial products are regulated in New Zealand for energy efficiency — via Minimum Energy Performance Standards (MEPS), Mandatory Energy Performance Labelling (MEPL), or both. The regulated products and their requirements include:

**MEPS and MEPL (both):**
- Air conditioners (which include heat pumps)
- Clothes dryers
- Electronic displays / televisions
- Computer monitors
- External power supplies
- Household refrigerating appliances (fridges and freezers)

**MEPL only:**
- Clothes washers
- Dishwashers

**MEPS only:**
- Ballasts for fluorescent lamps
- Chillers
- Close control air conditioners
- Compact fluorescent lamps
- Computers and laptops
- Distribution transformers
- Electric storage water heaters
- Gas water heaters
- Linear (tubular) fluorescent lamps
- Refrigerated cabinets
- Set top boxes

## Upcoming

Air conditioners above **65 kW** (which include heat pumps) will be regulated for energy efficiency from **1 May 2026**.

*Note: MEPS generally set a maximum energy use or minimum efficiency when a product is tested to a particular Standard; MEPL requires the Energy Rating Label to be displayed. Each product links to detailed requirements on the EECA site. This list reflects the products shown at the retrieval date and changes over time as the E3 Workplan progresses.*

# VEEEL compliance — motor vehicle traders

Motor vehicle traders are required to display Vehicle Emissions and Energy Economy Labels.

## Who is a motor vehicle trader

Anyone identified as a Motor Vehicle Trader under sections 7 and 8 of the Motor Vehicle Sales Act 2003 who carries on a motor-vehicle trading business. You're also treated as a trader if you sell more than **six** vehicles in a 12-month period, or import more than **three** in 12 months, unless you can prove it's not for financial gain. As a registered trader, you must also display a Consumer Information Notice (CIN) on all used vehicles you sell to the public.

## Finding the information

Use EECA's Vehicle Emissions and Energy Economy Label Generator — entering the VIN, registration plate, chassis number, model code, or Japanese export certificate model code. If information isn't available for the vehicle, you've met your legal obligations; printing and displaying the "Not Available" label shows buyers and EECA's compliance team that you made a genuine effort.

## Displaying the label

If information is available, display the label on the vehicle so it's clear, prominent and obviously relates to that vehicle — easy to read from a reasonable distance, and either firmly attached and visible from outside, or on an information stand next to the vehicle. Once the vehicle is sold, the label can be removed (a "sold" sign avoids confusion). For online listings, display the make, model, emissions and energy economy as written text alongside the listing, from the first time of listing.

## Exemptions and penalty

You don't need to provide the information when selling to another motor vehicle trader, or advertising in a magazine or newspaper. Failure to comply can result in a penalty of up to **$5,000 per offence** (per vehicle).

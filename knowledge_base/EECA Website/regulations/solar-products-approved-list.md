# Approved list — solar products

EECA maintains a list of residential solar products it has approved based on their efficiency and demand flexibility, to help homeowners choose rooftop solar panels, inverters and batteries with greater confidence. Because solar information can feel overwhelming or overly technical, the list makes it easier to identify high-quality products.

## What the list covers

The products on the list have been assessed by EECA and meet the criteria in its technical specification. Currently the list covers solar photovoltaic panels and inverters; the range will continue to grow, and batteries will be added soon.

## Future-proofing

As technology develops, solar systems, appliances and the electricity grid will increasingly communicate with each other to manage flexible electricity use — smoothing peak demand and lowering power bills. Products on the list meet the specifications to participate in this system, so they're future-proofed. Since solar panels typically last 25–30 years, ensuring installed systems are efficient and smart helps maximise the value they deliver over their lifetime.

## Technical specification

EECA's solar product technical specification sets minimum **voluntary** requirements covering efficiency and "smartness" (demand flexibility), along with methods for showing how products meet them. It was developed following consultation in September 2025 and is intended for suppliers of solar panels, inverters and batteries. The focus is residential systems, but the requirements also apply to small commercial systems, and the specification may evolve as technology and standards develop.

## For suppliers

Suppliers of solar products in New Zealand can register with EECA and apply to have their products added to the approved list if they meet the technical specification.

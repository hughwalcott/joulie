# VEEEL compliance — new vehicle distributors

New vehicle distributors must display Vehicle Emissions and Energy Economy Label information. The rules apply no matter where the promotion takes place, and to all vehicles on display. You don't need to display the label if a vehicle is promoted or offered for sale only to other motor vehicle traders.

The relevant regulations are the Energy Efficiency (Vehicle Fuel Economy Labelling) Regulations 2007 and the Energy Efficiency (Vehicle Energy Economy Labelling) Amendment Regulations 2022. Failure to comply can result in a penalty of up to **$5,000 per offence** (per vehicle).

## Finding and printing the label

Use EECA's Vehicle Emissions and Energy Economy Label Generator (entering the VIN, registration plate, chassis number or model code) to check whether information is available. If it is, print a label in colour or greyscale to display on the vehicle.

## How to display it

The label must be clear and prominent, obviously relate to the vehicle for sale, and be easy to read from a reasonable distance — either firmly attached to the vehicle and clearly visible from the outside, or displayed on an information stand next to the vehicle.

If you run a motor-vehicle trading business in New Zealand, you probably need to be registered as a motor vehicle trader.

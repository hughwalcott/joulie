# E3 compliance — registering your product

Regulated products must be registered before being sold in New Zealand, so EECA can ensure they meet MEPS and MEPL requirements. The registration system is part of the trans-Tasman E3 Programme, and once registered, products appear on the E3 public registration database.

## Who registers, and what counts as a product

Only New Zealand importers and manufacturers can register products in New Zealand, and you must register each product you wish to sell. A "product" is defined by its model number, brand, and energy performance characteristics. Products with multiple model numbers but the same brand and energy performance characteristics can be registered together (a "family registration"); in some specific circumstances, products with differing energy performance can also be grouped.

## How to register

Complete the digital form on the E3 website, including all required fields. The regulator will approve the registration or come back to you if there are issues. Once approved, keep a note of the record ID — you'll need it to provide sales data. A test report is the prescribed form (in PDF) at the back of the applicable Standard; where no prescribed form exists, the hard-copy registration forms can serve as a substitute test report.

## Registration statuses

- **Draft** — started but not yet submitted.
- **Submitted** — sent to the regulator.
- **Returned** — sent back to the applicant because changes are required.
- **Approved** — the product is registered.
- **Variation Approved** — a variation to the registration has been approved.
- **Expired** — applies in Australia only; New Zealand registrations don't expire.
- **Archived** — the product was re-registered before expiry and now has a new record (which carries the current status).

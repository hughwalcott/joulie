# E3 compliance — providing annual sales data

Importers and manufacturers of regulated products must provide sales data to EECA each year.

## Getting set up

Apply for a sales data tool account (allow 5 working days for processing; each company can have only one account). A guide is available to help you navigate the tool.

## What and when to report

Enter your data for the previous year after **1 April** but no later than **1 August** each year. EECA assesses the data and either accepts it or contacts you for correction. For each product, provide the total number sold between **1 April and 31 March** of the previous year — for example, data provided after 1 April 2021 covers sales from 1 April 2020 to 31 March 2021. You also need to advise EECA of any products you're no longer selling. Where products share a family registration (same brand and energy performance characteristics), you can report their sales together.

## How the data is used

EECA uses sales data to track the effectiveness of the E3 programme — how much energy is saved through improving product efficiency — and to track and publish market trends. The information provided is treated as sensitive and confidential.

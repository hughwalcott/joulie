# Energy Rating Label for televisions and computer monitors

The Energy Rating Label shows how much energy a television or computer monitor uses per year and gives a star rating for comparing efficiency against other TVs or monitors of the same size.

## How the rating is calculated

The annual energy consumption and star rating assume the TV or monitor is **on for 10 hours a day and in standby for 14 hours**, in the recommended "home viewing" mode.

- **Televisions** currently on the market generally display **1 to 8 stars**.
- **Computer monitors** generally display **1 to 9 stars**.

There is roughly a **20% reduction in energy consumption for each additional star**.

## Test standards

- Televisions are tested using AS/NZS 62087.1:2010 and rated using AS/NZS 62087.2.2:2011 (+A1+A2).
- Computer monitors are tested using AS/NZS 5815.1:2012 and rated using AS/NZS 5815.2:2013 (+A1+A2).

# Energy Rating Label for fridges and freezers

Residential fridges and freezers must have an Energy Rating Label supplied with them and displayed on the product in stores. The label shows how much energy the appliance uses per year and gives a star rating for comparing efficiency against other fridges or freezers respectively.

## Old vs new label — not comparable

A new Energy Rating Label has been introduced that's easier to read and consistent with other products. During the transition you may see either the old or new label on products for sale. **Unlike some other products, the annual energy consumption and star ratings cannot be compared between the old and new fridge/freezer labels** — the testing requirements and calculations changed to better reflect real usage (for example, the effect of opening and closing the door).

## How the new rating is calculated

The new label's annual energy consumption and star rating are calculated using the test standard assuming an ambient temperature of **22°C**, taking into account steady-state operation, defrost, and the extra energy required for door openings and cooling warm food. The new label also states total fridge/freezer volume, uses larger stars, and cites the updated test standard AS/NZS IEC 62552-3.

Star ratings for fridges and freezers on the market are between **1 and 6 stars**, with roughly an **18% reduction in energy consumption for each additional star**.

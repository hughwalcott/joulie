# How to comply with E3 product regulations

To legally sell, hire, lease, or provide hire purchase of regulated products in New Zealand, you must ensure the product meets the relevant requirements under the Energy Efficiency (Energy Using Products) Regulations 2002.

## The core requirements

- **Minimum Energy Performance Standards (MEPS)** — a product with MEPS requirements must perform at least as efficiently as the relevant Standard requires. MEPS are product-specific but generally set a maximum amount of energy a product may use, or a minimum efficiency when tested to a particular Standard.
- **Mandatory Energy Performance Labelling (MEPL)** — a product with MEPL requirements must have the Energy Rating Label attached when on display, and supplied with each of those products you sell.

Alternatively, a product may qualify if it meets the conditions under the **Trans-Tasman Mutual Recognition Agreement** — but manufacturers and importers are still required to provide sales data to EECA.

## Penalties

Failure to comply with the Regulations can result in a penalty of up to **$10,000 per offence** (per product sold). It is your responsibility to make sure you comply.

## Role-specific guidance

The programme sets out obligations for retailers and for manufacturers/importers separately. Manufacturers and importers must test products (or obtain a test report) to the applicable Standards, register each regulated product through the E3 system before it can be sold, and provide annual sales data to EECA. Minimal-quantities exemptions are available in specific circumstances (e.g. products subject only to MEPL where no more than 50 units of a model are imported or manufactured).

# Compliance and monitoring (Vehicle Emissions and Energy Economy Labelling)

Motor vehicle traders, vehicle distributors and private sellers all have a duty to comply with the Vehicle Energy Economy Labelling Regulations. EECA takes a consistent approach to improving compliance and to any compliance action.

## How EECA encourages and enforces compliance

- **Education and support** — presentations and newsletters to industry and consumer programmes to aid understanding of the Act; publication of responses to compliance-monitoring activity, offences, contraventions and adverse decisions.
- **Assisted compliance** — monitoring such as labelling surveys and site visits.
- **Proactive compliance** — responding to those who opportunistically or deliberately contravene the legislation.
- **Responsive interventions** — proportionate sanctions, warnings or directions.
- **Enforcement** — failure to comply can result in a fine of up to **$5,000 per offence** (per vehicle).

## Monitoring in practice

EECA monitors compliance through labelling surveys and targeted online checks, and visits motor vehicle traders each year to confirm they're meeting Vehicle Emissions and Energy Economy Labelling requirements. In 2020/21, EECA surveyed 650 on-site motor vehicle traders and recorded a compliance rate of **89.7%** — a marked improvement on the previous year's 77%, attributed to enhanced market monitoring, dealer education and more efficient monitoring.

# E3 compliance — minimal quantities exemption

In specific circumstances, importers and manufacturers may be exempt from two steps in the usual compliance process, and retailers may be exempt from one step (the MEPL requirements). Only importers and manufacturers can apply for an exemption; retailers should ask their supplier for a copy of the approved exemption if it applies to products they sell.

## Exemption from product registration

Available where **all** of the following apply:

- the product is subject only to MEPL (this applies to clothes dryers, clothes washers, and dishwashers), **and**
- no more than **50 units** of the same model will be imported into or manufactured in New Zealand by anyone, **and**
- you have applied for a minimal quantities exemption, **and**
- it has been approved.

## Exemption from Mandatory Energy Performance Labelling (MEPL)

Available where no more than **50 units** of the same model will be imported into or manufactured in New Zealand, subject to application and approval on the same basis.

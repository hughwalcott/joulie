# E3 compliance — information for manufacturers and importers

If your product is covered by the Energy Efficiency (Energy Using Products) Regulations 2002, you must complete the full set of compliance steps. In summary:

1. **Check whether your product is covered** by the Regulations.
2. **Meet MEPS and/or MEPL requirements** — test your products, or obtain a test report, to the applicable Standards to confirm they meet New Zealand's Minimum Energy Performance Standards and/or Mandatory Energy Performance Labelling requirements.
3. **Register each regulated product** through the E3 system before it can legally be sold in New Zealand (with exemptions for minimal quantities).
4. **Display and supply the label** — the seven products with MEPL requirements must have the label correctly displayed and supplied with each one.
5. **Provide annual sales data** to EECA for each regulated product.

Related steps are covered on their own pages: registering your product, providing sales data, and applying for a minimal-quantities exemption.

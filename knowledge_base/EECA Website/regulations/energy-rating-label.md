# How to use Energy Rating Labels and star ratings

Energy Rating Labels make it easy to compare how much energy similar household appliances use, and how much they cost to run, before you buy.

## When and where the label applies

The Energy Rating Label applies to certain products under the Energy Efficiency (Energy Using Products) Regulations 2002, and those products must display the label when offered for sale. EECA administers label compliance in New Zealand; the label also applies in Australia, having been developed under the trans-Tasman Equipment Energy Efficiency (E3) programme.

## What's on the label

The label features a **star rating**, along with the product's **annual energy consumption in kilowatt-hours (kWh) per year**, based on assumed usage and standardised tests. It also shows the product's **Test Standard**. Products are tested to the Standards cited in the Regulations under defined test conditions, and both the star rating and the annual energy consumption are derived from those test results.

## Who uses it

- **Importers and manufacturers** use it to promote the energy efficiency and running costs of appliances.
- **Retailers** use it to help customers choose more efficient products with lower running costs.

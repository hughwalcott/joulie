# Solar product technical specification

EECA created a technical specification to support the uptake of efficient solar technology in New Zealand. It sets a set of **minimum voluntary requirements** covering efficiency and "smartness" (demand flexibility), along with methods for showing how products meet them. Suppliers of solar products can apply to have their products added to EECA's approved solar products list if they meet the specification.

## Scope

The specification is intended for suppliers of **solar panels, inverters and batteries**. It was developed following consultation in September 2025 (with input from bodies including the Clean Energy Council). The focus is residential systems, but the requirements also apply to small commercial systems, and the specification may evolve as technology and standards develop.

## Some of the requirements

- **Inverters** — the inverter requirements cover inverters used for solar and/or batteries, across single-phase and three-phase configurations, and include string, micro, optimised, grid-tied, hybrid and grid-forming inverters.
- **Demand-flexibility communication** — products should support OpenADR (version 2.0 or newer) so they can respond to external signals.
- **Standby power** — a low standby draw (of the order of less than 10 W) is specified.
- **Batteries** — a round-trip efficiency of at least **85%**.

The current published version is V1.2 (available as a downloadable PDF). Feedback on the specification can be sent to star@eeca.govt.nz.

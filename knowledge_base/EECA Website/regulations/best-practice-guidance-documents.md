# Best practice guidance documents

EECA supports the development of best-practice guidance to help businesses, industry and consumers make informed decisions about energy-efficient technologies. These documents help readers navigate complex technical requirements and industry best practices, and are developed in partnership with Standards New Zealand (typically as Publicly Available Specifications, PAS).

## Types of guidance

- **Installation guides** — recommendations for the safe and effective installation of energy-efficient technologies.
- **Industry guidance** — resources for suppliers, manufacturers and installers on meeting high standards of product and system performance.

## How to use the guidance

- **Check the guidelines** — download a document to understand the recommended specifications for different energy-using products.
- **Look for approved products and resources** — use EECA's approved lists to find products that meet the standards in these documents, and use the easy checklists included in the guidelines.

All of EECA's best-practice guidance documents are available for download at no cost.

## Available documents

- **Best practice guidance for smart homes** — helping New Zealanders make their homes smarter and more efficient, saving money and energy.
- **Best practice guidance for residential electric vehicle chargers** — enabling safe, energy-efficient EV charging at home.
- **Best practice guidance for commercial electric vehicle chargers** — enabling safe, energy-efficient EV charging in commercial settings.
- **Best practice guidance for procuring energy-efficient biomass boilers** — helping businesses and organisations choose efficient biomass boiler systems.
- **Best practice guide for residential solar PV and battery storage** — helping consumers get the most from residential solar PV and battery storage.

# EV Smart Charger Approved List

EECA maintains an approved list of residential and commercial EV chargers that meet its smart-charger specification, to help New Zealanders identify and buy chargers that use smart technology and are energy efficient. Inclusion in the list relates only to compliance with EECA's smart-charger specification.

## Why smart chargers

- **Speed of charge** — smart EV chargers can be at least **3× faster** than the default 3-pin plug supplied with an EV.
- **Cost savings** — charging at home off-peak is currently like buying petrol at around **$1.60/litre**, including road user charges (depending on your retailer); a smart charger can be set to charge preferentially during off-peak periods, avoiding expensive peaks.
- **Smart-grid ready** — as markets develop to incentivise off-peak use, smart chargers will be able to react to grid signals, reducing or pausing charging when electricity is expensive and ramping up when it's cheaper (with an opt-out). These services are being developed and trialled.
- **Safety and load balancing** — smart chargers respond automatically during peak household use to avoid overloading the home's supply, and present fewer hazards.
- **Convenience** — set-and-forget charging ensures the car is ready when needed.
- **Cleaner** — off-peak charging draws on a higher proportion of renewable generation, reducing reliance on fossil fuels at peak times.
- **Access electricity deals** — retailers can advise on lower-cost off-peak pricing and what EV or charger is needed to access it.

## Two-way (bidirectional / V2X) chargers

Two-way chargers can both charge a vehicle and export stored energy from the vehicle back to a building or the grid, letting EVs act as flexible storage that supports household or business energy use and wider grid stability. This technology is emerging in New Zealand, but availability and vehicle compatibility are limited and standards are still developing; EECA is running a trial to understand how they operate and how consumers use them.

**Important:** EECA does not currently have a specification for two-way (bidirectional) functionality. Chargers on the additional list of recommended two-way chargers have been assessed against EECA's broader smart-charger specification and meet the smart-charging requirements *as well as* having two-way capability — but the bidirectional (V2X) functionality itself has not been assessed or endorsed. Seek advice from your supplier and installer on electrical safety and grid connectivity for two-way chargers.

## Why it matters

As EV numbers grow, smart, efficient chargers help meet rising electricity demand by shifting charging to off-peak times — deferring expensive network upgrades, helping keep energy bills lower, and supporting New Zealand's emissions-reduction commitments.

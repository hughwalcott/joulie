# Energy Rating Label for clothes washers

Clothes washers carry Mandatory Energy Performance Labelling (MEPL) requirements — an Energy Rating Label must be supplied with each product and displayed in retail stores. Clothes washers also require a **Water Efficiency Label**. (Unlike clothes dryers, clothes washers do **not** have MEPS requirements.)

## What's covered

MEPL covers residential clothes washers: horizontal-axis (front loader) and vertical-axis (top loader) single-bowl machines, twin-tub units, and the washing function of combination washer/dryer units — including both drum-type and non-drum-type (impeller or agitator) machines. It does not apply to commercial clothes washers that aren't sold into the residential market.

## Regulatory notes

Amendments to the NZ Energy Efficiency (Energy Using Products) Regulations 2002 come into force on **1 May 2026**, aligning with Australia's GEMS (Clothes Washing Machines) Determination 2015 and making relatively minor updates to testing and labelling. Australia has since introduced a new Determination and a new-look Energy Rating Label (which displays program time); the standards cited in that 2024 Determination can't be used to demonstrate compliance in New Zealand. For registration, New Zealand accepts test reports to AS/NZS 2040.1:2005 (with amendments) and AS/NZS 2040.2:2005 (with A1). Combination washer/dryers must comply with the requirements for both clothes washers and clothes dryers, and importers/manufacturers must provide annual sales data. Queries: star@eeca.govt.nz

# Energy Rating Label for heat pumps and air conditioners (Zoned Energy Rating Label)

The Zoned Energy Rating Label tells you which heat pumps and air conditioners will perform best in the different climate zones of New Zealand and Australia. It shows a star rating of up to **10 stars** for both heating and cooling, so you can pick a model suited to your local climate.

## Portable units

Labelling requirements for portable heat pumps and air conditioners came into force on **1 July 2021**:

- **Portable double-duct units** display at least half a star.
- **Portable single-duct units** display zero-star ratings.

Although single-duct portable air conditioners release cool air, they aren't efficient at cooling a space. This is because the unit draws air from inside the room and vents it outside, reducing the air pressure in the room; that pressure drop pulls warm air back inside from other areas, counteracting the cooling effect.

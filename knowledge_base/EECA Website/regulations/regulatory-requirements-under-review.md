# Regulatory requirements under review

EECA regularly reviews and develops product energy efficiency regulations, largely in step with Australia through the trans-Tasman E3 programme (working with the Australian Department of Climate Change, Energy, the Environment and Water, DCCEEW). The following requirements are currently under review or in development:

- **Heat pump water heaters** — regulatory policy options are being considered to improve their energy efficiency in Australia and New Zealand. (HPWH technology can reduce household hot water energy use by 50% or more; following consultation in late 2025, Australia's Energy and Climate Change Ministerial Council agreed to introduce Minimum Energy Performance Standards and labelling for HPWHs.)
- **Distribution transformers** — EECA and DCCEEW have consulted on policy options to improve their energy efficiency across both countries.
- **Electronic displays** — at the end of 2024, the Australian Energy and Climate Change Ministerial Council agreed to introduce new minimum energy performance and labelling requirements for electronic displays.
- **Space heating** — EECA and DCCEEW have consulted on a Space Heating Comparison Methodology.
- **LED lamps** — on 28 April 2018, the COAG Energy Council Ministers, alongside then NZ Minister of Energy and Resources Megan Woods, agreed to introduce minimum standards for LED lamps aligned with European Union regulations.

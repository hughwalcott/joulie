# VEEEL compliance — private sellers

If you're selling a vehicle **online**, you must show Vehicle Emissions and Energy Economy Label information in your web advertising. You can also choose to display the label on the vehicle itself, but that isn't compulsory for a private sale.

## Finding the information

On some trading websites the information appears automatically when you enter the vehicle's number plate. Otherwise, use EECA's Vehicle Emissions and Energy Economy Label Generator to find the information or print a label. If the information isn't available for the vehicle, you've met your legal obligations.

## What to display

If the information is available, display the vehicle make and model, emissions, and energy economy as written text alongside the vehicle listing.

## Exemption

You don't have to provide the information if you're advertising the vehicle in a magazine or newspaper.

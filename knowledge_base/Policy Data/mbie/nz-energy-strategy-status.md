# Does New Zealand have an overarching national energy strategy?

**This is unsettled — an area to hedge, not state firmly.**

MBIE spent 2024–2025 developing a **draft New Zealand Energy Strategy** alongside a set of sub-strategies (a published **Wood Energy Strategy**, a **Government Statement on Biogas**, and a **draft Geothermal Strategy**). However, reporting from around **May 2026** indicated the Government had **stepped back from producing a single overarching strategy document**, in favour of these narrower, sector-specific statements.

So as at mid-2026 the position is best described as: New Zealand has (or is developing) several **sector-specific energy strategies/statements**, but **not** a single overarching national energy strategy — and this has been contested, with some business groups expressing concern about the loss of a unifying strategy and the investment certainty it would provide.

## Related current workstreams

Energy policy direction is instead being expressed through: the Government Policy Statement on Energy, Electrify NZ (generation/consenting), the dry-year reliability obligation, the Gas Transition Plan, and the broader "Energy Package" (including LNG import facility procurement).

## ⚠ Currency note

This is one of the most in-flux items in New Zealand energy policy and should be **confirmed directly with MBIE before being treated as settled**. Present it as: sector sub-strategies exist; a single overarching national energy strategy was stepped back from around mid-2026; the situation is contested and may change. Avoid stating either "NZ has a national energy strategy" or "NZ has abandoned energy strategy" as a firm fact.

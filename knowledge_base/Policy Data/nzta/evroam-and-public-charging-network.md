# EVRoam and the national public EV charging network

## What EVRoam is

EVRoam is NZTA's live national database of publicly accessible EV charging locations. Charging network operators submit data on each charger's location, connector type, power, availability and status to EVRoam, which then feeds the public-facing NZTA EV charging station map. This gives drivers a single, consistent, up-to-date view of the public charging network across all operators, rather than having to check each operator separately. The map is updated from EVRoam continuously; NZTA's static network maps are refreshed monthly.

## National guidance for public EV charging infrastructure

NZTA publishes national guidance to help councils, charging network operators and other planners deliver a consistent, reliable, nationwide network of public chargers. It covers where chargers are best located, the technical standards they should meet, and how to plan for a growing EV fleet as part of the wider transport system. NZTA is part of a cross-agency working group developing a national plan for public EV charging infrastructure, providing strategic direction and giving industry greater certainty about how to prepare for future demand.

## The 10,000-charger target

The Government committed, in the Government Policy Statement on land transport 2024, to delivering **10,000 public EV chargers by 2030** (subject to cost-benefit analysis) — roughly one public charger for every 40 EVs. NZTA and the charging map are the mechanisms for tracking progress toward that target. (Investment detail and progress figures are covered in the Beehive/Ministry of Transport records.)

## ⚠ Currency note

Charger counts change constantly. For the live count and locations, use NZTA's EV charging station map (fed by EVRoam) and EECA's public EV charger dashboard, rather than any fixed figure.

# Road user charges (RUC) for electric and plug-in hybrid vehicles

Road user charges are how New Zealand ensures every vehicle contributes to building and maintaining the roads. Petrol vehicles contribute through fuel excise duty (FED) included in the pump price; vehicles that buy little or no petrol pay RUC instead, in prepaid distance blocks (usually 1,000 km). RUC revenue goes into the National Land Transport Fund. RUC is governed by the Road User Charges Act 2012.

## Who pays RUC, and the current rates

- **Battery electric vehicles (BEVs)** — powered by electricity only. Light BEVs (under 3,500 kg) pay **$76.00 per 1,000 km**, the same rate as a light diesel vehicle.
- **Plug-in petrol hybrids (PHEVs)** — pay a **reduced rate of $38.00 per 1,000 km**, because their owners also pay fuel excise duty on the petrol they buy.
- **Plug-in diesel hybrids** — pay the full **$76.00 per 1,000 km** rate.
- **Non-plug-in ("self-charging") petrol hybrids** — do **not** pay RUC, because they pay fuel excise at the pump.

An administration fee applies each time a RUC licence is bought (about $12.44 online, $13.71 through an agent, at time of retrieval). RUC is prepaid: the owner buys distance in advance, the odometer records distance travelled, and purchased distance must be kept ahead of actual distance (checked at Warrant of Fitness).

## When and why the EV exemption ended

Light EVs were exempt from RUC from 2009 to encourage early uptake. The exemption was always intended to end once EVs reached about 2% of the light vehicle fleet — a threshold reached in 2024 (around 100,000 light EVs on the road). **Light EVs and PHEVs became subject to RUC from 1 April 2024.**

## ⚠ Currency note

RUC rates are set by government and reviewed periodically. The $76 (BEV / light diesel / plug-in diesel hybrid) and $38 (plug-in petrol hybrid) per-1,000-km rates and the admin fee were current as at retrieval (September 2026). Always confirm the live figures at nzta.govt.nz before quoting them as current. Some third-party sources incorrectly quote a $53 PHEV rate; the NZTA figure is $38 for plug-in petrol hybrids.

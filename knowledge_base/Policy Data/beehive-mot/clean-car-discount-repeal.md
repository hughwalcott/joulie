# The Clean Car Discount and its repeal

## What the Clean Car Discount was

The Clean Car Discount was a government scheme that applied **rebates** to newly registered low- and zero-emission vehicles and **fees** to high-emission vehicles, based on each vehicle's CO2 emissions. It ran in two phases:

- **1 July 2021 – 31 March 2022:** rebates only (for eligible low- and zero-emission vehicles).
- **1 April 2022 – 31 December 2023:** both rebates and fees ("feebate" model).

## Repeal

The incoming government repealed the scheme via the **Land Transport (Clean Vehicle Discount Scheme Repeal) Amendment Act 2023**, and it **ended on 31 December 2023**. No rebates or fees apply to vehicles registered from 1 January 2024.

## Current position on EV purchase support

There is currently **no equivalent national purchase rebate or subsidy for EVs**. Government support for transport electrification has instead focused on:

- expanding public charging infrastructure (investment and the 10,000-by-2030 target);
- regulatory settings at the Commerce Commission and Electricity Authority to support EV charging and economy-wide electrification; and
- the broader Road User Charges reform intended to move all vehicle types onto a consistent, distance-based charging model over time.

The **Clean Car Standard** (a separate policy setting CO2 limits on imported vehicles) is distinct from the Clean Car Discount and was not repealed by this change — keep the two separate.

## ⚠ Currency note

Whether a purchase incentive exists is a policy area that can change with government decisions. Confirm there is still no national EV purchase rebate before stating so as current fact.

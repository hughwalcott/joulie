# Electrify NZ

Electrify NZ is the Government's package of measures (announced August 2024) to speed up the build-out of renewable electricity generation and transmission, on the basis that electrifying transport, heating and industry only cuts emissions if there is enough renewable electricity to meet the extra demand.

## Main elements

- A **Fast-track Approvals** regime for nationally/regionally significant projects, including major renewable generation and transmission. The Government has noted that close to **one in five fast-track applications are renewable energy projects**.
- **RMA amendments** to shorten renewable-energy consenting timeframes and lengthen consent durations (up to **35 years**) and lapse periods.
- Stronger, more directive **National Policy Statements** for renewable electricity generation and for transmission.
- A **new offshore wind permitting regime**.
- Modernised **Commerce Commission / Electricity Authority** settings to support economy-wide electrification, including EV charging infrastructure.

## Consenting cost rationale

A 2022 NZ Infrastructure Commission (Te Waihanga) study, cited in support of the reforms, found the average cost of consenting infrastructure had risen about **70% since 2014**, with developers spending an estimated **$1.29 billion a year** nationally on consenting.

## Progress (as at 2026)

The Government reports the reforms have restored investment confidence, stating the **volume of new generation built between 2023 and 2025 was double the volume built in the previous eight years**, with wind, solar, geothermal and battery projects coming online. New rules requiring electricity retailers to offer **time-of-use pricing** have also come into effect.

**Offshore wind:** originally not operating, with a regime targeted for around mid-2025. As at mid-2026 the Government has **passed the law establishing the offshore wind regulatory regime**, and feasibility permits were expected to be able to be issued within months — a significant step beyond the 2024 position, though no offshore wind is generating yet (developers eye mid-2030s commissioning).

## ⚠ Currency note

Electrify NZ spans legislation and regulation that is still being implemented. Offshore wind status in particular is moving quickly (regime law passed 2026; feasibility permits imminent) — confirm the current stage (permits open, granted, construction) before stating it. Treat consenting-reform outcomes as in-progress.
